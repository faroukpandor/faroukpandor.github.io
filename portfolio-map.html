02 — EVIDENCE & GOVERNANCE RULES

Shared evidence standards.

How routing stays honest

The personal site documents the person and evidence; enterprise destinations handle customer execution where ready; client and employer systems remain segregated and explicitly labelled.

Evidence labels

VERIFIED — primary evidence available. DOCUMENTED — supported by a source record. SELF-REPORTED — stated but not independently verified. HISTORICAL — retained as history. UNVERIFIED — requires confirmation.

Specialist boundary

Regulated legal, veterinary, clinical, financial or engineering sign-off belongs exclusively with qualified providers. Coordination and preparation stay clearly scoped. Inspect live repository status in the Control Dashboard or test routing and landed economics in the Sourcing & Demand Workbench.
