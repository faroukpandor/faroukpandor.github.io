# Farouk Pandor — Personal Brand & Professional Portfolio

Canonical public publishing site for Farouk Pandor.

This repository is the presentation layer for a broader professional and enterprise ecosystem. It routes visitors to evidence, professional records, research and canonical enterprise repositories rather than duplicating enterprise products.

## Deployment
Designed for GitHub Pages at https://faroukpandor.github.io/ using a static, provider-portable architecture with no runtime server, database or paid SaaS dependency.

## Trust rules
- Do not present historical concepts as active enterprises without confirmation.
- Do not present client or third-party assets as Farouk-owned.
- Distinguish documented evidence from self-reported or unverified claims.
- Do not imply professional licensure or regulated authority without current evidence.
- Keep enterprise-specific functionality in its canonical repository.


## Commercial opportunity and partner-programme hub

The homepage includes an evidence-led section for supplier channels, reseller/MSP programmes, procurement opportunities and qualified delivery partnerships. GFI Software is listed as **under assessment**, not as an approved partner. The section links to the official GFI application and the MOKORO opportunity assessment.

### Publication controls
- Confirm programme and territory eligibility directly with the provider.
- Do not claim authorisation, certification, appointment, margin, incentive or customer outcome without written evidence.
- Keep client/employer assets separate from Farouk-owned enterprises.
- Treat the personal site as a routing and presentation layer; keep enterprise-specific operational records in their canonical repositories.
- Re-check external programme terms before acting, since eligibility and commercial terms can change.
