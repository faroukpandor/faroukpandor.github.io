## Safe screening checklist

Proceed only after the original source provides enough information to verify:

1. The legal company or named client and a credible official website/domain.
2. An application page or recruitment contact published on that official domain.
3. A clear task description, expected volume, quality/rejection rules and actual compensation calculation.
4. Written payment terms, supported payment methods for Botswana, minimum payout and any fees.
5. Privacy and data-handling terms explaining what worker information is collected.
6. Confirmation that applicants are not required to pay registration, training, equipment, activation or withdrawal fees.

Do not provide passwords, one-time codes, account-recovery codes, bank login details, or identity documents to an unverified recruiter. Do not install unknown software, transfer money for an employer, receive and forward funds, or use a personal bank account to process payments. A request for upfront payment is a strong reason to stop.

Ask for the **original advertisement URL or screenshot showing the platform and poster/company name**. Verify the company and its official application route before spending time applying. If the source cannot be supplied, leave the lead unverified and move on.
