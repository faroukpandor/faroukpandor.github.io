# Opportunity Triage: “$120/Hour Remote Studies (Worldwide)”

**Captured:** 2026-10-16  
**Source supplied by Farouk:** Reddit post in r/workfromhomejobs, titled “Stop Scrolling: $120/Hour Remote Studies (Worldwide)”; poster u/wilson_wfh; comments direct applicants to Telegram username `@lindawfh`.  
**Decision:** Reject this specific recruitment route unless independently verified; retain the general category of legitimate paid research participation for further eligibility checks.  
**Confidence:** High confidence that the supplied post has material scam indicators; insufficient evidence to conclusively identify the poster or offer as fraudulent.

## What the supplied post claims

- Select remote studies allegedly pay US$120 per hour.
- It names several countries but uses the broad phrase “and more”; Botswana is not explicitly listed.
- It provides no named company, study title, official recruitment page, study protocol, participant terms, privacy notice or payment terms.
- The poster asks people to direct-message them and then sends them to a Telegram account, `@lindawfh`.
- A commenter notes the poster account is very new and calls the post a scam. That comment is a warning signal, not independent proof by itself.

## Risk assessment

| Signal | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Very high headline rate without a named sponsor or study | High-risk, unsubstantiated claim |
| Recruitment shifted to an individual Telegram account | High-risk; identity and company affiliation not established |
| No official application page or verifiable terms | High-risk; no way to confirm the actual opportunity |
| Broad international eligibility without a clear list | Eligibility for Botswana is unverified |
| New account and a scam warning in the supplied comments | Additional caution signal; not conclusive proof |

**Action on this specific post:** Do not message the Telegram account, send identity documents, provide banking details or OTPs, pay fees/deposits, purchase equipment, install unknown software, or perform “tasks” that require transferring money. Do not share passwords or account recovery codes. Reconsider only if a real sponsoring organisation, study details, official domain, privacy terms, payment terms and Botswana eligibility can be independently confirmed.

## General opportunity worth investigating

Paid research participation is a legitimate category in principle, but availability and income are not guaranteed. The safer route is to use official participant platforms and verify current country eligibility and payment methods before investing time.

### Official checks

- **Prolific participant eligibility:** https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445007-who-can-participate-in-studies-on-prolific  
  The current country list displayed by Prolific does not include Botswana. Its guidance says people in unsupported countries may join a waitlist, without guaranteed access. Do not misstate residence or use a VPN to bypass eligibility.
- **User Interviews supported countries:** https://support.userinterviews.com/hc/en-us/articles/51535822654099-Countries-where-you-can-participate-in-studies  
  Its current supported-country list does not include Botswana. Recheck the official list before applying.
- **Respondent country and recruitment information:** https://help.respondent.io/en/articles/5471299-what-countries-can-i-recruit-in-on-respondent  
  Its page lists Botswana among countries available for recruiting in some research contexts, but this does not by itself confirm that a Botswana resident can sign up as a participant or qualify for a particular study. Check participant onboarding and payment requirements directly.

These platform details were checked against official help pages on 2026-10-16 and may change. Do not treat a platform as viable until the participant registration process, supported residence, available study types, payout method and minimum payout have been verified.

### Low-cost validation checklist

- [ ] Identify the actual sponsoring legal entity and official website independently.
- [ ] Confirm the study exists through a company-domain page or independently contacted official support.
- [ ] Confirm Botswana eligibility in writing and the legal/payment requirements.
- [ ] Confirm compensation, expected duration, selection conditions, privacy terms and payment method before providing non-essential personal information.
- [ ] Never pay to receive work or unlock earnings.
- [ ] Record time spent, successful applications and verified net earnings; discontinue if opportunity cost exceeds realistic return.
