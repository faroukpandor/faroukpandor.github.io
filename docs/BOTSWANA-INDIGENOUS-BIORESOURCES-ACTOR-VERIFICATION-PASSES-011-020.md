# Indigenous Bioresources — Actor Verification Passes 011–020

**Research date:** 2 October 2026

## Pass 011 — NARDI institutional route

NARDI is a Botswana agricultural Research Technology Organisation with explicit mandates for R&D, commercialisation and technology transfer across the agricultural value chain. Its current structure includes Field Crops & Horticulture, Food Science & Technology, Agricultural Economics & Statistics, Natural Resources Management, Agricultural Engineering & Mechanisation, and Technology Transfer & Commercialisation. citeturn0search3turn0search4

**Opportunity interpretation:** NARDI is a potential institutional evidence/technology interface. No partnership, procurement relationship or access is assumed.

## Pass 012 — NUS actor map

NARDI's NUS programme explicitly names morama, lengana, mosukujane and kgengwe and aims at domestication, production and commercialisation. citeturn0search1

**Next evidence required:** named current projects, responsible researchers, locations, germplasm/seed status, trial results, production protocols and technology-transfer status.

## Pass 013 — Food-processing actor

NARDI's Food Science and Technology department conducts product/process development, food safety/quality work and commercialisable food technologies, with an explicit objective of transferring technology platforms to entrepreneurs. It also describes customised agro/food-processing systems and technology packages. citeturn0search2

**Commercial route:** customer problem → technical brief → NARDI capability → prototype/process → testing → entrepreneur/processor → market validation.

## Pass 014 — Food-safety evidence actor

NARDI identifies analytical work covering contaminants, adulterants, pesticides, mycotoxins, heavy metals, toxins, antimicrobial resistance and emerging pathogens, and says methods should support market-entry requirements. citeturn0search2

**Control:** never infer that a product is safe because the institution has testing capability; obtain product-specific results.

## Pass 015 — Agricultural economics actor

NARDI's Agricultural Economics and Statistics function is intended to validate economic viability and provide market intelligence. citeturn0search3

**Potential service opportunity:** research/intelligence coordination around indigenous-resource value chains, subject to actual requirements and institutional terms.

## Pass 016 — BITRI technology-transfer actor

BITRI's Technology Transfer Office manages IP and business development and documents licensing, assignment and spin-off/start-up mechanisms. It also states that technologies offered for licensing or assignment undergo BOBS accreditation before transfer. citeturn0search6

**Control:** technology listed by BITRI is not automatically available to Farouk.

## Pass 017 — BITRI electronics/IoT actor

BITRI's Electronic Communications Division describes locally developed electronics, wireless sensor networks, edge devices, gateways, dashboards and alerts for agriculture, water quality and environmental monitoring. citeturn0search8

**Potential application:** indigenous-crop trials, water monitoring, environmental conditions, processing equipment and traceability.

## Pass 018 — BITRI prototyping/manufacturing actor

BITRI's DPMP division offers product design, prototyping, fabrication, assembly and production, including 3D printing, machining, welding, CNC machining and laser cutting. It states that it supports external clients and SME product development. citeturn0search10

**Potential application:** prototype shell-breaking tooling, guards, fixtures, sensor enclosures or small processing components. This requires a defined engineering brief before feasibility can be assessed.

## Pass 019 — Current NARDI service-price evidence

NARDI published a 2026 pricing document for products and services, dated March 2026 and updated May 2026. citeturn0search0

**Use:** retrieve the actual document and map each relevant test/service to a product-development cost model.

**Control:** published institutional pricing is not necessarily the final cost for a specific project; verify scope, sample requirements, turnaround, taxes/fees and current applicability.

### Farouk role boundary

Farouk may legitimately operate as researcher, sourcing agent, coordinator, market-intelligence provider or commercialisation/orchestration intermediary where the customer, institution and contractual terms support it.

Do not represent Farouk as NARDI/BITRI staff, partner, agent, licensee, accredited laboratory, regulator, researcher of record or technology owner without explicit evidence.

## Decision rule

**Institutional capability is not customer demand.  
Capability is not availability.  
Availability is not quotation.  
Quotation is not delivery.  
Delivery is not outcome.  
Outcome is not repeatability.**
