# Africa Opportunity Research SOP

## Sequence
1. Detect signal.
2. Verify source.
3. Identify country and REC context.
4. Identify the actual problem.
5. Identify potential customer.
6. Identify existing suppliers/competitors.
7. Map value chain and logistics.
8. Test compliance.
9. Calculate economics.
10. Define smallest commercial experiment.
11. Seek customer commitment.
12. Deliver and document outcome.
13. Test repeatability.
14. Route to the appropriate Farouk-owned enterprise.
15. Productise only after evidence.

## Stop conditions
Stop or archive when:
- no identifiable customer;
- economics are negative without a justified strategic reason;
- compliance pathway is unclear for a regulated activity;
- evidence is stale or contradictory;
- opportunity depends on assumed government endorsement;
- supplier or delivery pathway cannot be verified.

## Principle
Research depth follows evidence. Do not create country reports merely to increase document count.
