## What problem we aim to solve

Farmers need reliable, timely, actionable agricultural support. Extension workers need practical ways to manage caseloads and follow up. Researchers and service providers need evidence and coordination. Market connections must be verified rather than promised. Our proposed contribution is to connect these parts while preserving the role of qualified people and public institutions.

## Canonical capability map

- **AgriSage360 / AGRISAGE:** farmer advisory, extension content, officer case triage, action plans and outcome tracking.
- **AgriSolutions / AGRISOLUTIONS:** field delivery, demonstrations, practical support and aggregation services where qualified and contracted.
- **Research Exchange Africa / RESEARCH-EXCHANGE:** evidence methods, baseline design, research quality and evaluation.
- **EverythingCity:** buyer/input/service discovery, evidence-graded listings and quote-led enquiries.
- **SmartFarm:** operational farm records and production workflows, only where integration ownership is confirmed.
- **AnimalHealth / Livestock Botswana:** livestock record templates and official-resource navigation, not diagnosis or treatment authority.
- **MOKORO Digital Growth Engine:** software delivery, automation, training and institutional implementation.
- **MOKORO Enterprise OS:** portfolio governance, ownership boundaries, stage gates, risks and economics.

## Evidence standard

Use the categories **VERIFIED**, **USER-REPORTED**, **INFERRED**, **PROPOSED** and **UNKNOWN**. A repository document is not proof that a capability is deployed; a deployed app is not proof of adoption; user satisfaction is not by itself causal impact; and an identified buyer is not proof of a current purchase commitment.

For public claims, report the target population, geography, period, method, denominator, limitations and evidence source. Do not claim increased yields, income, resilience, official endorsement, partnerships, certification or recurring revenue without supporting evidence.

## Execution priority

1. Audit existing code, documentation, test gates and deployments.
2. Choose one narrow farmer problem and a willing partner.
3. Establish baseline and service responsibilities.
4. Validate reviewed content, offline usability, consent and human fallback.
5. Run a small, measured pilot and publish limitations as well as findings.
6. Expand only after demonstrated user value, safety and viable delivery economics.

## Ownership and change control

The portfolio map is not an ownership certificate. Client and third-party assets remain isolated. No client production system should be changed without explicit authorisation. Link to canonical capability owners rather than copying whole products or datasets.

**Current claim:** we have documented a coordinated programme proposal. This does not establish that a pilot, integration, partnership or measured outcome exists.
