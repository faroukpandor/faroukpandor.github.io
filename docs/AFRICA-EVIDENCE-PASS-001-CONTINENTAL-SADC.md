## Commercial interpretation

These sources establish policy/institutional signals only. They do **not** establish customer demand, supplier capacity, price acceptance, profitability, procurement eligibility or government endorsement.

The next evidence pass must therefore move downstream into:
1. country-specific official sources;
2. actual buyer/customer evidence;
3. supplier/provider evidence;
4. landed economics;
5. compliance;
6. paid transaction or pilot evidence.

## Control
No ranking of countries is created. Research depth follows evidence.
