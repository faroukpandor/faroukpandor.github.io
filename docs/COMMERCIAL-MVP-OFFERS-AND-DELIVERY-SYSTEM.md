# Commercial MVP Offers & Delivery System

**Status:** Market-test architecture — not proof of established demand.

## Commercial doctrine

Convert broad capability into small, understandable, low-capital offers. Use: **Problem → Diagnostic → Deliverable → Price hypothesis → Delivery → Evidence → Review → Repeat/expand**. Do not build new platforms before manual/service demand is demonstrated.

## Primary Offer A — Business Operations Desk

**Customer:** SMEs and organisations needing reliable recurring administrative capacity.

**30-day pilot:** document/correspondence organisation; meeting/action registers; follow-up; quotation/proposal and tender administration; business-record organisation; monthly activity report.

**Boundary:** legal, tax, accounting, audit and formally reserved professional functions remain with appropriately qualified providers.

**SOP:** intake → baseline workload → establish registers/folders → process agreed tasks → record turnaround/rework → monthly report → continuation review.

**Recurring path:** pilot → monthly desk → operations desk → Mokoro automation assessment.

## Primary Offer B — Sourcing & Commercial Desk

**Customer:** buyers needing suppliers, products or services found, compared, quoted or coordinated.

**Sourcing Sprint:** requirement clarification; supplier/product search; comparable shortlist; specification/price comparison; source/evidence register; risks/unknowns; next procurement actions; optional supplier coordination.

**SOP:** capture requirement → acceptance criteria → multiple-source search → verify material claims → comparison → risk review → sourcing brief → coordination → outcome record.

**Commercial rule:** disclose commissions/referrals and never present unverified supplier, price, certification or delivery claims as fact.

**Recurring path:** sourcing sprint → recurring sourcing desk → supplier intelligence → procurement administration.

## Primary Offer C — Digital Operations & Automation

**Customer:** SMEs with repetitive manual workflows, scattered information or inefficient processes.

**Workflow Improvement Sprint:** current-state map; bottleneck diagnosis; smallest useful intervention; lightweight implementation; SOP/user guide; export/backup plan; handover/training; before/after measurement where feasible.

**SOP:** observe → select one bottleneck → map → quantify → design smallest intervention → build with portable/free-first tools → test → document fallback → measure → offer maintenance only when justified.

**Recurring path:** sprint → support → automation portfolio → digital operations retainer.

## Supporting Offer D — Production & Fulfilment Coordination

Activate when customer demand requires apparel/branding production coordination, embroidery/branding job coordination, supplier/equipment/operator coordination, production scheduling, QC records or fulfilment.

Present Farouk's actual management/coordination experience without implying ownership of client/employer assets. Keep this as supporting capability rather than the headline personal identity.

## Supporting Offer E — Research & Intelligence Sprint

A bounded research question produces a research brief, source register, structured findings, uncertainty/gap register, decision-oriented report and optional monitoring plan. CurrentTrace is infrastructure; the customer buys the research outcome.

Recurring path: research sprint → monitoring → sector watchlist → intelligence briefing.

## Supporting Offer F — Agricultural Field Support

Potential bounded services: farm-record setup, field observation/data collection, farm operational audits, agricultural project coordination, research fieldwork, input/logistics coordination and implementation support.

AgriSolutions is the physical-world layer; AgriSage/smartfarm support intelligence and digital workflows. Regulated animal-health diagnosis/treatment remains with appropriately qualified/registered professionals where required.

## Universal opportunity intake

Capture: customer/contact; organisation; problem; desired outcome; urgency; location; deadline; budget indication if volunteered; existing resources; specialist/regulatory requirements; confidentiality/data sensitivity; proposed offer; exclusions; next action; status.

### Qualification gate

Proceed when there is an identifiable customer and problem, meaningful outcome, feasible delivery, acceptable risk, plausible willingness/ability to pay and worthwhile economics.

## Evidence scoreboard

**Leads → conversations → qualified opportunities → proposals → paid pilots → revenue → direct delivery cost → contribution → hours → turnaround → rework → customer outcome → repeat purchase → referral → recurring intent.**

Repositories, prototypes and enthusiasm are not substitutes for paid demand evidence.

## Free-first acquisition

Use existing professional relationships, appropriate former/current business relationships, WhatsApp business conversations, LinkedIn, local SME/professional networks, referrals and useful problem-led content. Start with the customer's problem and a bounded pilot, not the entire portfolio. Avoid expensive advertising before evidence.

## First evidence experiment

Run one controlled 30-day test for each primary front door. Identify a real problem; agree a bounded paid or explicitly scoped pilot; document baseline; deliver manually; measure outcome; calculate contribution; ask about repeat purchase; then continue, change, pause or retire.

## Portfolio routing

**Farouk personal brand → customer/problem → offer → appropriate enterprise → specialist/provider where required → evidence → outcome → recurring relationship → reusable SOP/data/automation.**

## Implementation checklist

- Single opportunity/intake register.
- One SOP pack per primary offer.
- One-page pilot brief per primary offer.
- Pricing hypotheses and quote rules.
- Evidence capture fields.
- First reachable prospects.
- Manual pilots before new technology.
- 30-day review.
