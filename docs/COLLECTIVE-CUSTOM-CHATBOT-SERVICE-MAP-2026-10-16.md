# Collective Custom Chatbot Service Map
**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** Portfolio capability proposal; customer deployment and commercial readiness are assessed individually.

The portfolio should offer custom chatbots without cloning a chatbot product into every repository. **MOKORO DGE is the canonical technical and commercial delivery owner.**

| Enterprise | Role |
|---|---|
| MOKORO Digital Growth Engine | Customer discovery, chatbot UX, implementation, approved integrations, testing, handover and support |
| FAROUKPANDORCONSULTANT | FAQ/SOP preparation, business workflow mapping, content governance and administrative readiness; technical build routed to DGE |
| EverythingCity | Supplier/directory/marketplace-specific enquiry assistant only when real data and validated demand justify it; DGE owns generic implementation |
| AgriSage360 | Agriculture-specific knowledge and workflows, preserving its offline-first and veterinary/professional boundaries |
| MOKORO Enterprise OS | Governance, data/security standards, acceptance gates, commercial and repository boundaries |
| Client repositories | Isolated delivery with explicit authorisation; no cross-client copying of data, secrets or proprietary configuration |

## Minimum delivery standard
- Customer problem and success metric defined before build.
- Scope, content ownership, fee, exclusions, support and acceptance terms written down.
- Bot uses approved sources, discloses limits and escalates unsupported/high-stakes questions.
- No invented prices, stock, availability, policies or commitments.
- Privacy, data minimisation, retention/deletion, access control and platform/API terms checked.
- Secrets are never committed to source or exposed in browser bundles.
- Tests cover incorrect answers, prompt injection, stale content, outages, abuse and handoff.
- Human fallback, rollback, customer acceptance and ongoing content ownership are documented.
