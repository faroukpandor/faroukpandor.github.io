# Problem-First Homepage Contract

## Purpose

The homepage is the portfolio's front door, not an organisational chart.

## Primary question

Within a few seconds a visitor should understand:

**Can Farouk help me with a practical need or opportunity, and how do I start?**

## Required hierarchy

1. Plain-language problem statement
2. Simple call to action
3. Short explanation of what Farouk coordinates
4. Optional routes for people who want more detail
5. Professional evidence and portfolio deeper in the site

## Do not require

- knowledge of enterprise names;
- knowledge of commercial modes;
- knowledge of internal workflow states;
- understanding of the portfolio architecture;
- completion of a long form before starting.

## Interface principle

**Problem first → conversation → clarification → routing.**

The enterprise architecture remains behind the interface.

## Acceptance test

A first-time visitor should be able to answer:

- What can I ask Farouk about?
- Where do I start?
- Do I need to choose an enterprise?

Expected answers:

- Practical needs and opportunities across sourcing, business support, digital, agriculture, research and specialist coordination.
- Start with the simple enquiry.
- No. Start with the problem.
