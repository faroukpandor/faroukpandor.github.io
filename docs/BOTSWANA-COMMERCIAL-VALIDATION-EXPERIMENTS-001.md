# Botswana Commercial Validation Experiments 001

**Created:** 2026-10-02  
**Purpose:** Convert verified opportunity signals into low-capital customer-validation experiments.

## Evidence base

Government of Botswana publishes possible business opportunities across agriculture, agro-processing, construction materials, chemicals, ICT, paper, textiles/clothing, recycled materials and professional/ICT services. This is a signal source, not proof of demand. Source: https://gov.bw/doing-business/identified-possible-business-opportunities

Statistics Botswana publishes recurring monthly merchandise-trade and food/beverage import releases. July 2026 total imports were P7,064.4 million CIF and exports P5,160.6 million; Statistics Botswana states that trade statistics include import composition, principal commodity groups and trading partners. Source: https://www.statsbots.org.bw/merchandise-trade-statistics

## Experiment queue

### EXP-BW-001 — Business procurement sourcing
- Customer hypothesis: SMEs and organisations with recurring product/service requirements.
- Offer: requirement capture + supplier discovery + quotation coordination.
- Commercial mode: SOURCE-TO-ORDER / ORCHESTRATED PROCUREMENT.
- Capital rule: no inventory purchase before customer commitment.
- Evidence required: 10 real requirements; 3 quoteable requirements; at least 1 paid transaction.
- Routing: General Dealer/Sourcing + MOKORO where digital workflow is required.
- Stop: no identifiable buyer or economics do not work.

### EXP-BW-002 — Corporate merchandise/branding coordination
- Customer hypothesis: businesses, schools, organisations and events needing branded garments or promotional items.
- Offer: blank merchandise sourcing + artwork/specification coordination + production/branding coordination + delivery.
- Commercial mode: CUSTOM-TO-ORDER / ORCHESTRATED PROCUREMENT.
- Capital rule: customer deposit or confirmed order before production commitment.
- Evidence required: 10 enquiries; 3 quotations; 1 paid order; documented supplier/production performance.
- Ownership control: WCD production assets remain employer/client assets; do not represent them as Farouk-owned.
- Routing: Brandcraft / General Dealer / authorised production partner.

### EXP-BW-003 — Facilities/handyman referral coordination
- Customer hypothesis: property owners, tenants and organisations needing verified trades.
- Offer: requirement capture + provider matching + transparent referral/coordination + completion confirmation.
- Commercial mode: REFERRAL / COORDINATION.
- Capital rule: no stock.
- Evidence required: 10 leads; 5 completed jobs; provider performance records; transparent fee arrangement where applicable.
- Routing: provider/referral network.
- Stop: repeated complaints, unverifiable providers or unclear liability.

### EXP-BW-004 — Agricultural input sourcing
- Customer hypothesis: farmers requiring specific inputs, equipment or services.
- Offer: requirement diagnosis + source/quote comparison + coordination.
- Commercial mode: SOURCE-TO-ORDER.
- Capital rule: customer commitment before purchase.
- Evidence required: 10 requirements; 3 viable quotes; 1 paid transaction; supplier verification.
- Routing: AgriSage360/AgriSolutions + General Dealer.
- Compliance: product-specific permits/registration where applicable.

### EXP-BW-005 — Import-to-order vehicle sourcing
- Customer hypothesis: customers seeking specific used/pre-owned vehicles.
- Offer: requirement capture + verified vehicle sourcing + landed-cost estimate + transaction coordination.
- Commercial mode: IMPORT-TO-ORDER.
- Capital rule: no purchase without customer commitment and verified supplier terms.
- Evidence required: 3 qualified customers; 3 verified vehicle quotations; at least 1 completed transaction.
- Mandatory economics: vehicle price, freight, insurance, duties/taxes, clearing, registration/compliance, local delivery, FX and coordination costs.
- Routing: AutoLink.
- Control: supplier listing price is not automatically Botswana landed price.

## Validation protocol

For every enquiry record:
1. Customer identity/category.
2. Exact requirement/specification.
3. Quantity/frequency.
4. Required date/location.
5. Budget or acceptable price range where voluntarily supplied.
6. Existing supplier/provider and pain point.
7. Evidence of urgency.
8. Sourcing options.
9. Estimated total cost.
10. Proposed customer price.
11. Contribution after all known variable costs.
12. Compliance.
13. Customer commitment.
14. Outcome.

## Decision states

HYPOTHESIS → SIGNAL → CUSTOMER-VERIFIED → QUOTED → CUSTOMER-COMMITTED → PAID/PILOT → DELIVERED → REPEATABLE → RECURRING.

Negative outcomes must be recorded as evidence rather than hidden.

## Acquisition channels

Existing Facebook pages, WhatsApp Business, professional contacts, existing business relationships and relevant public channels should be treated as distributed acquisition assets. Do not create new channels until existing ones have been audited for audience, relevance, permissions and conversion.

## Rule

The purpose of this experiment queue is not to prove that a market exists. It is to discover whether specific customers will pay for specific outcomes under viable economics.
