# Enterprise Claims Readiness Initiative

**Status:** Draft operating programme; not a certification, legal opinion or proof that any claim is currently substantiated.  
**Purpose:** Help portfolio businesses and client enterprises either prove a claim, build the operational capability behind it, or replace it with narrower truthful wording.

## Canonical implementation guide

The detailed playbook is being developed in the MOKORO Digital Growth Engine PR branch:
[Enterprise Claims Readiness & Evidence Playbook](https://github.com/faroukpandor/MOKORO---Digital-Growth-Engine/blob/research/botswana-licensed-hawker-value-pilot-2026-10-18/docs/ENTERPRISE-CLAIMS-READINESS-PLAYBOOK.md).

That playbook defines a status ladder, risk tiers, evidence-pack schema, claim-family proof requirements, a 30-day implementation sequence, register template, go/no-go gate and AI/staff workflow controls. The linked file is on an open draft PR branch and is not yet a merged or published service promise.

## Enterprise operating rule

No material public claim is approved by copywriting alone. Each claim must have:
- exact published wording and every channel where it appears;
- a named owner, defined scope and risk tier;
- current, traceable evidence and a reproducible test or measurement method;
- controls and operational records showing the capability is actually maintained;
- limitations and exclusions visible to customers;
- accountable approval, review/expiry date and rollback/remediation owner.

If the proof is missing, either pause the claim or state the current maturity precisely: proposed, designed, implemented, tested, operationally evidenced or independently assured.

## First 30-day execution sequence

1. **Days 1–5:** inventory high-risk claims across all channels; assign owners; pause obviously unsupported absolutes.
2. **Days 6–10:** create evidence packs and predeclare tests/metrics/thresholds; approve safe interim wording.
3. **Days 11–20:** implement missing controls and instrumentation; test negative paths, exceptions, failures and representative conditions.
4. **Days 21–25:** run a controlled pilot; collect operational evidence over a meaningful period.
5. **Days 26–30:** approve only evidence-backed wording, align all channels, schedule reviews and retain rollback capability.

The dates are a planning cadence, not a promise that legal compliance, certification or reliability targets can be achieved in 30 days.

## Initial acceptance criteria

- Zero Tier 1 claims published without accountable approval and current evidence.
- Every live material claim has an owner, scope, evidence location and review date.
- Quantified outcome claims have a baseline, denominator, measurement window and source.
- Claims with expired evidence are automatically queued for review or removed.
- AI-generated marketing content is checked against approved claim-register wording before publication.
- Each high-risk claim has a failure response and a named person authorised to pause it.

## Boundaries

This initiative does not grant permission to test third-party systems, change client repositories, process personal data, contact customers or deploy changes. Those actions require the relevant authority and scope. This document does not certify any portfolio company or its products.
