# Botswana Literature Evidence Pass 004 — Value Chains and Commercialisation

**Research date:** 2 October 2026

## New synthesis

The deeper literature search shows a recurring implementation gap between documented biological/agricultural resources and dependable products or services. The recurring interfaces are processing, quality control, market access, information, climate adaptation, extension, logistics and commercial coordination.

### High-value evidence-to-enterprise chains

1. Indigenous resource → authentication → sustainable supply → processing → quality control → buyer.
2. Ethnoveterinary knowledge → species evidence → safety/efficacy research → veterinary pathway → farmer outcome.
3. Farm problem → sensor/data source → offline workflow → alert/decision → measured productivity or loss outcome.
4. Climate evidence → farm-specific risk → advisory/action → outcome measurement.
5. Research finding → unresolved implementation gap → customer → specialist partner → paid pilot.

## Specific evidence implications

The 2026 Batswana ethnoveterinary review records 116 plants across nine livestock-condition categories, while only about 52% had empirical information on biological effects, safety and phytochemicals. This creates a research-prioritisation opportunity, not permission to market treatments. citeturn0search0turn0search1

## Non-negotiable distinction

Literature evidence does not establish market demand, product-market fit, regulatory approval, willingness to pay or profitability. Every commercial route must independently pass customer, supplier, economics and compliance gates.
