## Decision rule

Sell a bounded service using verified existing capability before building a new product. Prices below are proposed test prices in BWP, not market research, quotations, guaranteed earnings or evidence of willingness to pay. Confirm scope, client need, access, delivery time, costs, applicable tax and client approval before quoting. Do not claim a service is available until its delivery checklist and required tools have been tested.

## First three candidates to validate

### A. Website & Digital Presence Health Check
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 750–1,500; estimated 3–5 working days after receiving access/materials.
- **Buyer/problem:** SME with a website that may be hard to use, outdated, poorly presented or losing enquiries.
- **Deliverables:** review up to 10 key pages; mobile/usability pass; contact-path and broken-link checks; page-title/meta review; basic accessibility signals; prioritised findings and 30-minute handover.
- **Exclusions:** penetration testing, guaranteed search rankings, paid tools, implementing fixes, uptime guarantees or legal compliance certification.
- **Client inputs:** approved URL, business goals, correct contact details, permission for any authenticated checks.
- **Acceptance:** report identifies each finding, evidence/location, impact, priority and suggested next action.
- **Readiness gate:** complete one sample audit against a test/public site; verify repeatable checklist and report template; do not imply automated coverage where checks are manual.

### B. Business Operations & Digital Asset Audit
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 1,000–2,500; estimated 3–5 working days for one small business and one agreed workflow.
- **Buyer/problem:** owner lacks a clear view of digital assets, lead flow, ownership, risks or practical next steps.
- **Deliverables:** asset/rights register based on supplied and authorised material; one workflow map; prioritised risks and quick wins; 30-day action plan.
- **Exclusions:** legal/IP opinion, financial audit, penetration test, regulatory certification or review of systems without explicit authorisation.
- **Client inputs:** named scope, authorised URLs/accounts/files, contact person and any restrictions.
- **Acceptance:** every material finding has evidence, confidence/status, impact and an actionable recommendation.
- **Readiness gate:** use a reusable intake, scope and evidence template; keep client-confidential data out of public repositories and AI tools unless expressly approved.

### C. SME Workflow & AI Opportunity Assessment
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 750–1,500; one 60–90-minute discovery session plus written recommendations.
- **Buyer/problem:** repetitive admin or enquiry work consumes time, but the right automation is unclear.
- **Deliverables:** one current-state process map; bottlenecks; manual/low-cost/AI options; data/privacy and failure-mode notes; recommended pilot and success metric.
- **Exclusions:** implementation, paid subscriptions, regulated decisions, guaranteed savings or any assumption that AI is necessary.
- **Client inputs:** workflow owner, sample data with personal/confidential fields removed, current tools and constraints.
- **Acceptance:** recommendations compare no-code/manual options with automation and specify a measurable test.
- **Readiness gate:** create a standard discovery questionnaire and sample report; calculate benefits from client-supplied measurements rather than invented percentages.

## Additional candidates — do not market until scoped and checked

### D. Website Care & Monthly Change Plan
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 500–1,200/month for a defined small site.
- **Scope:** agreed basic availability checks, one scheduled content change, link/contact-path spot check and monthly status report.
- **Excludes:** hosting/subscriptions, emergency recovery, redesign, security monitoring promises and guaranteed response times unless separately agreed.
- **Gate:** written access/security process, backup responsibilities, change approval and service-level boundaries.

### E. One-Page Lead-Generating Website
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 1,500–3,500.
- **Scope:** one responsive landing page, client-approved content/assets, one CTA, basic metadata and handover.
- **Excludes:** e-commerce, custom backend, paid integrations, hosting and ongoing maintenance.
- **Gate:** test build/deployment path, acceptance checklist and client content/rights approval.

### F. Local Search & Business Profile Improvement Pack
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 900–2,000.
- **Scope:** audit of one eligible business profile, business information and on-page local signals; review-response guidance.
- **Excludes:** guaranteed rankings, reviews, lead volumes or platform verification.
- **Gate:** verify eligibility and account authority; obtain approval before changing profile information.

### G. WhatsApp Enquiry & Lead-Tracking Setup
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 750–1,800.
- **Scope:** approved click-to-chat path, intake questions, lightweight lead tracker and follow-up templates.
- **Excludes:** bulk unsolicited messaging, unofficial API automation and unapproved customer-data transfers.
- **Gate:** test the actual handoff and tracking workflow; verify privacy, consent and deployed-tool status. The existing MOKORO client-acquisition source is local-first and deployment must be verified before promising hosted functionality.

### H. Small Workflow Automation Implementation
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 1,500–5,000 after scoping.
- **Scope:** one bounded, low-risk workflow, test cases, handover and rollback notes.
- **Excludes:** subscriptions, high-risk data processing, regulated decisions and ongoing support unless approved.
- **Gate:** approved system access, threat/data review, test evidence, rollback and cost approval before implementation.

### I. Tender & Supplier Readiness Check
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 750–2,000 for one tender or one supplier-readiness checklist.
- **Scope:** compare client-provided documents with published requirements; deliver gap list, checklist and action plan.
- **Excludes:** legal advice, paid registration, certification, eligibility guarantees or promise of award/funding.
- **Gate:** verify requirements against the issuing authority and current notice; date-stamp sources and preserve unknowns.

### J. Supplier / Product Sourcing Shortlist
- **Proposed test price:** BWP 1,000–3,000 for one defined category and shortlist.
- **Scope:** evidence-linked shortlist, comparison criteria and supplier verification questions.
- **Excludes:** guarantee of supplier legitimacy, stock, pricing, import clearance or delivery; freight, duties, samples and due diligence are separate.
- **Gate:** verify identity, current offer, terms and conflicts; disclose representation transparently and never take hidden margins.

## Initial order of operations

1. Test candidates A, B and C with a small set of relevant prospective buyers; do not publish all ten as equally ready.
2. Prepare a sample deliverable using public/synthetic data and measure actual preparation time.
3. Check whether the buyer problem is urgent, who controls the budget, willingness to pay and what alternative they use today.
4. Quote only after scope and delivery cost are understood; state exclusions and payment/acceptance terms in writing.
5. Record every prospect interaction and outcome in the authorised lead tracker; do not claim outreach or sales until actually completed.
6. Promote an offer only after delivery steps, access requirements, evidence standards and a realistic support boundary are documented.

## Evidence and stop rules

- No customer validation, paid pilot, revenue or deployment is claimed by this document.
- A proposed price is a test hypothesis, not a verified market rate.
- Do not use client/employer/third-party code, data, credentials or case studies without permission.
- Do not buy tools or subscriptions without explicit budget approval.
- If no buyer signal emerges after a small, relevant outreach test, revise or pause the offer rather than building more software.
