## Strategic thesis

Build a Botswana-first, SADC-capable commercial intelligence and transaction-orchestration capability. Sell verified outcomes—supplier discovery, procurement coordination, buyer/supplier matching, operational workflows and market-entry research—before building additional software. Automate only recurring bottlenecks demonstrated by paid or otherwise evidenced demand.

## Shared operating rules

- Existing assets and relationships before new brands, repositories, platforms or paid subscriptions.
- Validate demand before development; revenue before feature expansion.
- No speculative inventory, supplier deposits, paid APIs or unapproved expenditure.
- No unapproved client/third-party repository, data, website or system changes.
- No unsupported claims about qualifications, partners, product efficacy, certification, registration, market size or buyer commitments.
- Keep customer and supplier records confidential and out of public Git.
- Distinguish VERIFIED, USER-PROVIDED, INFERRED and UNVERIFIED information.
- Human approval remains mandatory for quotations, regulated decisions, commitments and consequential fulfilment.
- Measure contribution after direct costs; gross spread is not profit.
- Preserve repository ownership, licences, provenance, data rights and enterprise boundaries.

## Immediate validation sequence

Select one existing buyer requirement; verify a matching supplier; obtain dated written price and fulfilment terms; calculate full contribution; record the result and go/no-go decision. Do not build a new scanner or chatbot before completing this test.

## Legal and technical baseline

Botswana's Data Protection Act, 2024 commenced on 14 January 2025. Apply privacy-by-design, lawful processing, transparency, minimisation, access control, retention and security to lead and supplier records. Verify current WhatsApp platform pricing/eligibility and Cloudflare limits from official documentation before customer commitments.
