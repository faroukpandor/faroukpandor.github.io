## Non-negotiable controls
1. Policy alignment never substitutes for market proof.
2. No political preference inference, endorsement or election prediction.
3. No government endorsement or procurement eligibility is implied.
4. No unverified supplier, customer, inventory or regulatory claim becomes fact.
5. Client/employer/third-party assets remain clearly separated from Farouk-owned assets.
6. Commercial economics must include logistics, taxes/duties/fees and coordination costs where applicable.
7. Every material claim has provenance and retrieval date.
8. Every automated workflow has a manual fallback.
9. Research depth follows evidence.
10. The next pass must build on the previous evidence rather than merely restating it.

## Current authoritative baseline
The AU confirms 55 Member States. SADC confirms 16 Member States. SADC RISDP 2020–2030 has three principal development pillars: Industrial Development and Market Integration; Infrastructure Development; and Social and Human Capital Development, with cross-cutting areas. SADC also identifies agro-processing, mineral beneficiation and pharmaceuticals among priority industrial areas and identifies regional value-chain development as part of its industrialisation agenda.

This baseline is context only. Commercial validation remains transaction-based.
