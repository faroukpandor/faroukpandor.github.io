# Africa Procurement Evidence Pass 004 — Discovery and Compliance Controls

**Verified:** 2026-10-02

## Purpose

Convert procurement-related source signals into a controlled discovery workflow without treating procurement plans or tenders as guaranteed business.

## Botswana

### PRC-BW-001 — PPRA annual procurement-plan search
- Source: Botswana PPRA Procurement Management System.
- Fact: the system provides search over annual procurement plans by ministry and procuring entity.
- Critical limitation: the source explicitly states that planned procurement may be revised or cancelled and that an annual procurement plan is for planning/alert purposes, not an invitation to tender or bid notice.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- Use: early opportunity discovery only.
- Source: https://ipms.ppadb.co.bw/openProcumentPlan

### PRC-BW-005 — Example controlled procurement demand
- Source: Government of Botswana, Central Medical Stores.
- Fact: an emergency once-off medicines procurement notice invited registered CMS suppliers to offer medicines available locally or supplyable within 30 days; the notice stated that least-cost selection would be used and shorter lead time would receive preference among relevant quotes.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- Use: demonstrates the type of concrete requirement that can be captured in the opportunity ledger.
- Compliance gate: only registered/eligible suppliers should be considered; regulated pharmaceuticals must not be sourced/resold without the required authorisations.
- Source: https://gov.bw/emergency-once-procurement-medicines

## Procurement-to-commercial workflow

**DISCOVER → VERIFY NOTICE → IDENTIFY ELIGIBILITY → IDENTIFY CUSTOMER/PE → IDENTIFY REQUIREMENT → SUPPLIER CAPABILITY → PRICE/LEAD TIME → TAX/COMPLIANCE → BID/QUOTE ONLY IF AUTHORISED → DELIVER → RECONCILE → RECORD OUTCOME**

### Hard controls

- Annual procurement plan ≠ tender.
- Tender ≠ award.
- Award ≠ guaranteed margin.
- Public-sector requirement ≠ Farouk eligibility.
- A tender may require licences, registration, local-content status, tax compliance, bid security, technical capacity or other conditions.
- Regulated goods require additional compliance before sourcing or resale.
- No bid should be submitted in Farouk's name without explicit authority and a verified legal/operational capacity to perform it.
- Referral/facilitation fees or commissions must be transparent to the relevant parties.
- Do not claim government endorsement or access.

## Next action

The next procurement pass should extract a **small number of live, eligible, commercially relevant notices** from official systems and route them only if:
1. the notice is current;
2. eligibility is understood;
3. the exact requirement is documented;
4. a capable supplier/provider is identified;
5. economics are viable;
6. compliance is feasible;
7. the responsible Farouk-owned or authorised partner asset is identified.

If these gates cannot be met, record the notice as research-only and stop.
