## Botswana

### BOT-EVD-001 — Current national planning baseline

- Fact: Botswana's NDP system has operated since independence; NDP12 is described by NPC as the second plan implementing Vision 2036 and intended to run April 2025–March 2030.

- Commercial use: planning-context tag only.

## Evidence discipline

1. A national plan is a policy signal, not a customer order.
2. An investment-promotion page is a sector/opportunity signal, not proof of investability or profitability.
3. Trade deficits are not automatically unmet commercial opportunities; product-level demand, competition, pricing and logistics must be tested.
4. Procurement portals identify possible opportunities but do not establish eligibility, award, or customer commitment.
5. No source in this pass establishes Farouk ownership, supplier exclusivity, inventory ownership, government endorsement, or procurement eligibility.

## Next evidence actions

- Botswana: move from sector-level signals to product-level import data, customer enquiries, supplier quotations and landed economics.
- Namibia: inspect actual procurement/opportunity records and product-level trade categories before proposing cross-border experiments.
- Cross-border: document customs, tax, permits, route, payment and delivery requirements before quoting customers.
- Do not expand country coverage merely to increase document count.
