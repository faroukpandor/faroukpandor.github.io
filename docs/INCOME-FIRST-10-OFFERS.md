**Status:** Draft for review and customer validation; not yet published as a live offer page.  
**Owner/channel:** Farouk Pandor / MOKORO and consulting service channel, subject to final brand, capability and rights review.  
**Market starting point:** Botswana SMEs and organisations; expand only where delivery and buyer evidence support it.  
**Price status:** All BWP figures below are **proposed test prices**, not verified market rates or guaranteed income. Confirm scope, willingness to pay, delivery time, tax/fees, support burden and client requirements before quoting.

## Commercial rules

- Sell outcomes and bounded deliverables, not vague “AI transformation”.
- Use the existing asset or a manual process first; do not build new software before demand is demonstrated.
- Give a written scope, client responsibilities, exclusions, acceptance criteria, delivery date and payment terms.
- Request a deposit or milestone payment where appropriate; do not begin work that depends on unpaid third-party licences or subscriptions.
- Never guarantee rankings, sales, tender awards, funding, security, compliance or business outcomes.
- Use only authorised access and client-approved data. Do not place passwords, API keys, confidential customer data or tender documents in public repositories.
- Do not claim services are available until the required tools, skills, access, and delivery checklist have been verified.

## Offer 01 — Website & Digital Presence Health Check

**Proposed test price:** BWP 750–1,500 · **Turnaround:** 3–5 working days

**Best-fit buyer:** A small business with a live website but uncertain usability, visibility or enquiry performance.

**Included**
- Review one website, up to 10 agreed pages.
- Check mobile layout, broken links, page titles/meta, contact information, obvious content inconsistencies, enquiry path and basic accessibility signals.
- Identify visible performance or technical concerns without representing this as a penetration test.
- Deliver a prioritised report: critical fixes, quick wins and a 30-day action list.
- One 30-minute handover.

**Client supplies:** URL, business goals, target customer, approved factual information and any relevant analytics they choose to share.

**Not included:** Implementing fixes, paid SEO tools, penetration testing, legal/accessibility certification, guaranteed search rankings or guaranteed leads.

**Acceptance test:** Report covers the agreed pages, separates observed facts from recommendations, includes evidence/examples, and ranks actions by impact and effort.

**Natural next offer:** Website Care Plan or Local Search Improvement Pack.

**Prerequisite:** Confirm access/security process, backup responsibility, response window and escalation route before accepting a site.

## How to launch these offers without spreading effort too thin

1. Treat all ten as a **catalogue to test**, not ten products to build or ten campaigns to launch at once.
2. First check which offers can be delivered safely using existing tools and current capability. Mark others **not yet offerable** until the gap is closed.
3. Prepare a concise one-page sales sheet for each verified offer and a common intake/quote/delivery template.
4. Choose the initial outreach subset using customer access, urgency, low delivery cost, proof of competence and repeat-business potential.
5. Contact relevant prospects individually; log objections, actual price responses, meetings, proposals, deposits and delivery effort.
6. Review after the first 10–15 relevant conversations or earlier if strong evidence appears. Change the scope/price or stop an offer if the evidence is weak.
7. Publish only offers that have passed capability, delivery, privacy and pricing checks. Do not claim case studies or outcomes without permission and evidence.

## Required offer-readiness status

Use one of these statuses for each offer:
- **Draft:** wording exists; delivery process not verified.
- **Ready for a paid pilot:** scope, capability, delivery checklist, price hypothesis and terms have been reviewed.
- **Validated:** real buyer feedback supports the problem and offer; evidence and date recorded.
- **Sold:** written agreement/order confirmed.
- **Delivered:** acceptance evidence recorded.
- **Paid:** payment actually received.
- **Paused / not offerable:** missing capability, permissions, demand or viable economics.
