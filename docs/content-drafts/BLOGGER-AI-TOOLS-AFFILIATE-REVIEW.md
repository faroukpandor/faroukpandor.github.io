## Fix these points before using the post as affiliate-program evidence

The article currently opens with a generic “may contain affiliate links” disclosure, and makes broad claims that each item is “fully verified,” independently assessed against user reviews and regulatory guidance, and meets “FTC Compliance.” Applying to an affiliate program does not make links active or approved. Keep a disclosure only when actual affiliate links appear, and label the specific commercial recommendation. Do not present an article as legally compliant, independently audited, or hands-on tested unless that is specifically established.

The article also cites large market-size and commission-rate figures. Keep only figures with a current, credible source and defined scope; otherwise remove them. Do not imply that US regimes such as HIPAA or FDA review generally apply to all tools or to Botswana users. Explain jurisdiction only where relevant and supported.

## Suggested replacement introduction

> # A practical guide to free-to-start AI and productivity tools
>
> This guide helps readers compare tools for specific tasks such as research, writing, organisation and small-business workflows. It is informational—not a security audit, legal or clinical review, or a claim that every product has been tested hands-on. For each tool, I will distinguish current details checked against the provider’s documentation from personal testing and from anything that remains unverified. Free-plan limits, prices and features can change; confirm them with the provider before relying on them.
>
> The right tool depends on the task, device, internet access, privacy needs and total cost after any free allowance ends. Start with a low-risk task. Do not upload confidential, sensitive, health, financial or client information unless you are authorised and have checked the provider’s current terms and settings.

## Disclosure rule

- **No active affiliate link in the article:** remove the blanket “may contain affiliate links” notice.
- **Approved programme and a live tracked link:** disclose clearly before or beside the specific recommendation; state that a qualifying purchase may earn a commission. Use any exact programme-required statement as well.
- Never treat an application as acceptance, a normal product link as a referral link, or a commission as proof of testing or suitability. Do not claim blanket legal compliance.

Before restoring the article, check every product’s official product, price, terms and privacy pages; date-check changeable information; label actual testing honestly; remove unsupported market, performance, safety, security and certification claims; and assess whether a Botswana reader can access, pay for and get support for the service.
