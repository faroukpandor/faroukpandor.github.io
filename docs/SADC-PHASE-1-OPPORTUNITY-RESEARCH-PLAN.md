# SADC Phase 1 Opportunity Research Plan

## Why SADC first

Botswana is the operating laboratory. SADC is the first regional expansion because its RISDP 2020–2030 is organised around Industrial Development and Market Integration, Infrastructure Development, and Social and Human Capital Development. SADC also identifies agro-processing, mineral beneficiation and pharmaceuticals among priority industrial areas and is developing regional value chains. [Official SADC sources: https://www.sadc.int/pillars/regional-indicative-strategic-development-plan-2020-2030 and https://www.sadc.int/pillars/industrial-development-market-integration]

## Initial countries
Botswana, South Africa, Namibia, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Angola, Malawi, Tanzania, Lesotho, Eswatini, Mauritius, Seychelles, Madagascar, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Comoros.

## Research dimensions
For each country:
- national development plan;
- budget;
- investment priorities;
- procurement;
- import/export patterns;
- industrial/agricultural value chains;
- logistics corridors;
- digital/business services;
- historical executive/advisory/ envoy architecture;
- current market evidence.

## Commercial experiments
Prioritise experiments that can be executed from Botswana with low capital:
- sourcing;
- referral/facilitation;
- digital services;
- provider coordination;
- corporate merchandise;
- agricultural support;
- market intelligence;
- selected import-to-order opportunities.

No country receives a commercial ranking. Opportunities are compared by evidence completeness and transaction readiness only.
