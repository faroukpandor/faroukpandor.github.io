# Indigenous Bioresources — Laboratory, Standards and Regulatory Passes 021–030

**Research date:** 2 October 2026

## Pass 021 — BOBS testing

BOBS provides chemistry, microbiology and other product testing for raw materials, semi-finished and finished products. Its stated purposes include conformity, composition/quality, banned-substance detection, labelling claims, performance and safety. Its laboratories operate under ISO/IEC 17025; selected scopes are SADCAS-accredited. citeturn0search0turn0search1

**Implication:** testing should be selected from the actual product specification and applicable standard, not from a generic “natural product” checklist.

## Pass 023 — BoMRA regulated-product boundary

BoMRA's Quality Control Laboratory provides scientific testing and evaluation of medicines, medical devices and related regulated products. Its current laboratory page states ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and WHO Good Practices for Pharmaceutical Quality Control Laboratories as quality frameworks. citeturn0search3

A 2026 BoMRA guideline exists for registration of complementary medicines in Botswana. citeturn0search2

**Control:** an indigenous botanical is not automatically a medicine or complementary medicine; classification must be established before selecting a regulatory route.

## Pass 024 — Food route

Botswana's Food Control Act empowers regulation of food composition, hygiene, processing, packaging, storage, transport and testing. citeturn0search10

For prepackaged food, the Food Control Regulations require applicable labelling information including food name, ingredients, net contents, responsible business/address, country of origin, lot identification, date marking, storage instructions and instructions for use. Labels must not be false, misleading or deceptive. citeturn0search11

## Pass 025 — Manufacturing licence interface

Botswana's current industrial-licence guidance identifies Nutrition and Food Control as an approval interface for food and beverages and lists environmental-health, waste and other requirements for manufacturing applications. citeturn0search7

**Control:** product development and manufacturing-site compliance are separate workstreams.

## Pass 026 — Microbiology

BOBS has microbiology testing capability and SADCAS-accredited scopes including specified water microbiology tests. BoMRA states its microbiology unit is still under development for expanded regulated-product services. citeturn0search1turn0search3

**Implication:** select the laboratory based on matrix, test and accreditation/scope; do not assume every laboratory can perform every test.

## Pass 027 — Contaminants

For indigenous plant/food products, the evidence plan should consider product-specific contaminants such as microbial hazards, mycotoxins, heavy metals, pesticide residues and other relevant chemical hazards.

This is a **test-design hypothesis**, not a statement that a particular species is contaminated.

## Pass 028 — Authentication

Species identity, plant part, provenance and batch identity should be recorded before analytical interpretation. Where botanical authentication is material to a claim, use qualified taxonomic/herbarium or equivalent evidence.

**Minimum record:** scientific name, authority where relevant, local name, plant part, collection/production locality, date, collector/producer, batch ID, voucher/reference, photographs and chain-of-custody.

## Pass 029 — Stability and shelf life

A commercial food/botanical product needs a product-specific shelf-life rationale. Depending on product type, this can include moisture/water activity, microbial stability, packaging performance, oxidation/rancidity, chemical markers and storage conditions.

Do not infer shelf life from traditional use or from another species/product.

## Pass 030 — Claims and evidence matrix

Claims must be matched to evidence and regulatory classification:

| Claim class | Minimum control |
|---|---|
| Identity/origin | authentication + provenance |
| Nutritional | validated composition data |
| Food safety | relevant hazard testing + applicable controls |
| Shelf life | stability evidence |
| Cosmetic benefit | product-specific substantiation + classification |
| Traditional-use statement | accurately attributed evidence |
| Medicinal/complementary claim | applicable BoMRA route/evidence |
| Veterinary/animal-health claim | applicable veterinary/regulatory route |
| Commercial claim | current customer/transaction evidence |

## Commercial gate

**Resource → identity → product class → specification → applicable standard/regulatory route → test plan → laboratory capability/scope → result → corrective action → compliant prototype → customer validation.**

No medicinal, therapeutic, disease-treatment, safety or “approved” claim should be generated from literature alone.
