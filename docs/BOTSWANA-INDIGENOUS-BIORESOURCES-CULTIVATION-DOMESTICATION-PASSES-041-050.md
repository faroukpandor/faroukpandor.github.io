## Pass 041 — Morama domestication

## Pass 042 — Morama ecology and supply risk

NARDI describes NUS as often having limited distribution, low abundance and specialised habitats, with vulnerability to over-exploitation and poorly developed value chains. citeturn0search0

The 2025 morama survey confirms distribution in Botswana's sandy desert regions and documents harvesting/processing practices across Kweneng, Ghanzi, Southern and Central districts. citeturn0search4

**Implication:** wild-resource abundance cannot be treated as an unlimited raw-material inventory.

## Pass 046 — Moringa

Moringa is already represented in Botswana research and commercial activity, so the next question is not whether it can grow in Botswana but whether a particular production system can meet a verified specification at competitive cost.

**Required variables:** cultivar/source, propagation method, water, soil, planting density, harvest interval, biomass yield, drying method, labour, testing and buyer specification.

## Pass 047 — Propagation and planting material

For each species, the evidence register must distinguish:
**wild collection → seed collection → nursery propagation → vegetative propagation → farmer-saved seed → certified/verified planting material**.

Do not call material “certified,” “improved,” “registered” or “high-yielding” without documentary evidence.

## Pass 048 — Water and climate

NARDI identifies Botswana's rainfall as low and erratic and places climate-smart production and adaptation within its crop programmes. citeturn0search0

For NUS trials, water economics must be measured rather than assumed:
**rainfed performance → supplemental irrigation requirement → water volume → pumping/storage cost → yield response → gross margin.**

## Pass 049 — Soil/site adaptation

NARDI's NUS programme explicitly calls for research into establishment and adaptability to different environments. citeturn0search0

A commercial production protocol therefore needs site variables:
soil type/texture, pH, fertility, drainage, temperature, rainfall, elevation/local climate, pests/disease and management regime.

## Pass 050 — Sustainable harvesting and supply

NARDI's Natural Resources Management work links indigenous plants and underutilised crops to ecosystem services, sustainable use, biodiversity conservation and commercialisation of natural products. citeturn0search2

**Supply rule:** where a resource is wild or community-associated, commercial sourcing must establish provenance, sustainable harvesting, access/benefit-sharing requirements and continuity of supply before a customer promise is made.

## Commercial gate

**Species → provenance → propagation → site → production method → yield → input cost → quality → sustainable supply → processing → buyer specification → paid validation.**

No species becomes an assumed inventory source merely because it is indigenous or commercially interesting.
