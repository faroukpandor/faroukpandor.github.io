# Indigenous Bioresources — Post-100 Execution Queue v1

**Date:** 2 October 2026  
**Purpose:** convert the completed 100-pass architecture into a controlled operating queue.

## 1. Operating rule

The 100-pass programme is complete. The next phase is **execution, not document multiplication**.

No opportunity receives material capital merely because it has strong research evidence.

The minimum progression is:

**SIGNAL → VERIFIED REQUIREMENT → CUSTOMER-VALIDATED → QUOTED → COMMITTED → PAID PILOT → DELIVERED → MEASURED → REPEATABLE**

## 2. Immediate execution queue

### EX-001 — Evidence-dossier service
**Route:** Farouk Research & Intelligence  
**Capital exposure:** very low  
**Customer test:** one defined research question  
**Deliverable:** sourced evidence dossier + gap/opportunity memo  
**Commercial evidence:** paid engagement or explicit customer commitment  
**Fallback:** manual research workflow

### EX-002 — Sourcing/provider verification
**Route:** General Dealer / Sourcing  
**Capital exposure:** very low  
**Customer test:** one real requirement  
**Deliverable:** verified provider shortlist + comparable quotations + economics  
**Commercial evidence:** quote request followed by customer commitment  
**Fallback:** alternate providers + spreadsheet/manual register

### EX-003 — Laboratory/testing coordination
**Route:** Research + General Dealer coordination  
**Capital exposure:** low  
**Customer test:** one defined product/testing requirement  
**Deliverable:** test matrix, provider options, quotation and coordination  
**Control:** Farouk does not represent himself as the laboratory or technical authority

### EX-004 — Morama equipment requirement
**Route:** Sourcing / Commercialisation  
**Capital exposure:** customer-funded where possible  
**Gate:** actual processor requirement before sourcing  
**Deliverable:** specification + comparable equipment options + landed economics  
**No-go:** no verified requirement or unacceptable economics

### EX-005 — Indigenous ingredient B2B sample
**Route:** General Dealer + Research  
**Capital exposure:** low and preferably customer-funded  
**Gate:** named buyer specification  
**Deliverable:** verified sample/prototype + evidence + price  
**No-go:** buying stock speculatively

### EX-006 — Processing productivity test
**Route:** Indigenous Bioresources + AgriSage360 where agricultural data applies  
**Capital exposure:** capped pilot  
**Measure:** input → time → labour → energy → yield → waste → quality → cost/kg  
**Outcome:** determine whether a processing bottleneck is commercially actionable

### EX-007 — Offline monitoring pilot
**Route:** AgriSage360 + MOKORO  
**Capital exposure:** capped prototype only after customer problem verification  
**Target:** one measurable variable such as water, storage or processing condition  
**Success:** reliable measurement produces a useful operational decision

### EX-008 — Traceability pilot
**Route:** AgriSage360 / MOKORO  
**Capital exposure:** very low initially  
**Minimum:** source/input → batch → process → evidence → output → customer  
**Fallback:** CSV/manual batch records

### EX-009 — Technology-transfer scouting
**Route:** Research & Intelligence + MOKORO where digital  
**Capital exposure:** low  
**Gate:** actual customer problem + documented technology owner  
**Deliverable:** technology map, transfer conditions, economics and next-step recommendation  
**Control:** no claim of licensing availability without owner confirmation

### EX-010 — Recurring intelligence/sourcing desk test
**Route:** Farouk Commercial  
**Capital exposure:** very low  
**Gate:** evidence that a customer has a recurring requirement  
**Test:** defined monthly/quarterly service scope  
**Success:** paid recurring arrangement  
**No-go:** recurring billing without recurring customer value

## 3. Execution order

The queue should normally move from low-exposure service validation toward customer-funded coordination and only then toward physical prototypes or inventory.

This is an **execution sequence**, not a claim that one opportunity is intrinsically better than another.

- requirement

- decision
- review date

## 5. Customer commitment ladder

**ENQUIRY → REQUIREMENT → SPECIFICATION → QUOTE REQUEST → SAMPLE/TRIAL → DEPOSIT/PO → PAID PILOT → DELIVERED → REPEAT**

Do not promote an opportunity to “validated” based only on:
- research interest
- social engagement
- a supplier catalogue
- an institutional programme
- a government policy
- a published tender
- a directory listing
- an AI estimate
- a verbal statement without appropriate evidence.

## 6. Working-capital protection

Default rules:

1. Do not purchase speculative inventory where customer commitment can reasonably precede purchase.
2. Obtain customer deposits where commercially and ethically appropriate.
3. Record supplier deposits and outstanding balances.
4. Never treat customer deposits as profit.
5. Separate supplier money, customer money and Farouk contribution in the transaction record.
6. Include cancellation/refund and supplier-failure scenarios.
7. Quote validity must be explicit.
8. Never present supplier price as Botswana landed cost until landed economics are calculated.

## 7. Portfolio routing contract

**Research evidence** → Research & Intelligence  
**Physical sourcing/resale** → General Dealer / Sourcing  
**Agricultural operational workflow** → AgriSage360  
**Digital automation/data** → MOKORO  
**Branding/marketing after validation** → relevant Brandcraft capability  
**Market-entry coordination** → Farouk Commercial  
**Biological-resource evidence** → Indigenous Bioresources research layer

Client/employer/third-party assets remain clearly separated from Farouk-owned assets.

## 8. Resilience contract

For each live experiment maintain:

**Primary digital record → exportable CSV/JSON → local/manual fallback → alternate provider/system**

The experiment must remain reconstructable if:
- GitHub is unavailable;
- hosting fails;
- WhatsApp is unavailable;
- a supplier disappears;
- an API becomes paid;
- AI is unavailable;
- a device fails;
- a laboratory becomes unavailable.

## 9. KPI set

### Evidence
verified sources, unresolved gaps, verified actors

### Acquisition
enquiries, qualified requirements, source

### Commercial
quotes, commitments, paid pilots, contribution

### Delivery
lead time, completion, defects, customer outcome

### Repeatability
repeat orders, recurring customers, recurring revenue

### Resilience
fallback tests completed, export availability, alternate providers

## 10. 30-day execution cycle

**Week 1:** identify real requirements from existing acquisition channels and contacts.

**Week 2:** verify specifications, providers, evidence, compliance questions and economics.

**Week 3:** issue controlled quotes/proposals and seek customer commitment.

**Week 4:** deliver the smallest viable paid test where commitment exists; record realised economics and outcome.

At the end of each cycle:

**CONTINUE / REDESIGN / ROUTE / HOLD / ARCHIVE**

## 11. Immediate next action

Start with **EX-001: Evidence-dossier service** and **EX-002: Sourcing/provider verification** because both can be tested with minimal capital while exercising the same evidence, customer, supplier and commercial-control architecture required by the more capital-intensive indigenous-bioresource experiments.

The objective is not to produce more documentation. It is to produce the first **verifiable customer commitment and realised commercial outcome** that can then finance or justify subsequent experiments.
