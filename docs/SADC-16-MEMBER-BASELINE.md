## Research order
Each country requires a separate evidence record for:
- national development planning;
- current budget;
- investment agency;
- trade/customs;
- procurement;
- statistics;
- sector/value-chain signals;
- logistics;
- executive/advisory/representative history;
- customer/supplier evidence;
- commercial experiments.

No country is ranked. Research depth follows evidence.
