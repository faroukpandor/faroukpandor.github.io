# Public Content Governance

The public site is a presentation layer over the professional record and enterprise portfolio.

## Publication rules

1. Publish only claims supported by the professional record or clearly label them as self-reported/historical.
2. Separate completed qualifications from incomplete study.
3. Do not convert coursework into certification without certificate evidence.
4. Separate employment and client work from ownership.
5. Keep regulated professional decisions with appropriately qualified practitioners.
6. Link to canonical enterprise repositories instead of duplicating their claims.
7. Review dates, ownership, URLs and status before each major publication.

## Content pipeline

Source evidence → master professional record → evidence/status classification → public copy → UI → review.
