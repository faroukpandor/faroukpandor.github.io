# Botswana Institutional, Research, Innovation, Investment and NGO Opportunity Pass 001

**Verified:** 2 October 2026

## Purpose

Extend the opportunity intelligence system beyond Government procurement and national plans to include research institutes, investment/trade facilitation bodies, development organisations and civil-society/NGO ecosystems.

These records are opportunity/intelligence signals only. They do not establish contracts, grants, endorsement, eligibility, procurement access or customer demand.

## BIDPA — policy research and consultancy

BIDPA describes itself as an independent non-government policy research institute focused on policy research, analysis and capacity building. Its research areas include macroeconomics/development; trade, industry and private sector; human/social development; environment, agriculture/natural resources; and governance/administration.

BIDPA also undertakes demand-driven consultancy/advisory work for government, parastatals and other clients through tendering. Its published project inventory includes work involving CEDA, UNDP, ECF, BTO, NPC, BPC, PEEPA, DCEC, Ministry of Finance, UNDP, CEDA and agricultural value chains.

Commercial/research implication:
- monitor BIDPA tenders;
- map published research gaps to Farouk research/intelligence capability;
- identify legitimate subcontracting/partnering opportunities only where a live requirement and eligibility are verified;
- use publications as evidence inputs, not as proof of commercial demand.

Current tender page should be monitored separately.

## BITRI — applied technology, R&D and commercialisation

BITRI states that it develops technology-based solutions for diversification and practical national needs. Its current service areas include materials testing, digital transformation, energy/environment consulting, collaborative R&D, design/prototyping/production and innovation/commercialisation support.

BITRI's 2026–2031 strategy is described as placing disciplined R&D, societal impact and financial sustainability at the centre, with stronger partnerships across government, SOEs, academia and private sector.

BITRI also exposes capabilities relevant to:
- agriculture and environmental monitoring;
- wireless sensor networks;
- IoT/edge devices;
- data analytics;
- water-quality monitoring;
- electronics manufacturing;
- technology transfer;
- product design and prototyping.

Commercial/research implication:
- treat BITRI as a potential technology/R&D/technical partner or evidence source, not as a Farouk-owned capability;
- route IoT/automation and indigenous-bioresource technology concepts through evidence and consultation;
- investigate contract research, prototyping, testing, technology transfer and deployment pathways;
- never imply a BITRI partnership without documented authorisation.

## BITC — investment, export and market access

BITC currently promotes investment and export-development support. Published priority sectors include agriculture, automotive, cargo/freight/logistics, education, financial/business services, health, ICT, manufacturing, mining and tourism.

BITC export-promotion services include product development/adaptation, trade fairs, exporter education, manufacturer development, buyer-seller missions, market intelligence and market surveys.

Commercial/research implication:
- use BITC investment sectors as a structured opportunity taxonomy;
- use market-intelligence/export resources to identify buyer/market hypotheses;
- route export-ready Farouk/client products through appropriate channels;
- do not treat BITC promotion as buyer commitment or export eligibility.

## Development organisations and NGOs

### UNDP Botswana
Relevant areas include private-sector development, MSME competitiveness, supplier development, digitalisation, research/innovation, economic diversification, green economy, agriculture and resilience.

UNDP describes supplier-development work intended to position local firms in supply chains, and its Botswana portfolio includes private-sector, digital and economic-diversification work.

Commercial/research implication:
- monitor UNGM/UNDP procurement and consultancy notices;
- identify supplier-development, digital, agricultural, climate and research opportunities;
- distinguish consultancy procurement from programme beneficiaries;
- verify eligibility and closing dates before action.

### FAO
FAO Botswana identifies agro-processing, sustainable farming, youth participation in agri-food systems and sustainable smallholder livelihoods as relevant areas. FAO's Botswana work also includes agricultural standards, quality management, market access and value chains.

Commercial/research implication:
- monitor UNGM and FAO procurement;
- monitor agricultural value-chain, standards, training, digital/agtech and market-access opportunities;
- potential routing: AgriSage360/AgriSolutions, research/intelligence, sourcing and authorised specialist partners.

### GEF/UNDP small-grants ecosystem
Current 2026 funding discovery includes Botswana community-led themes such as sustainable agriculture, food security, conservation, low-carbon energy and sustainable livelihoods.

Commercial/research implication:
- grants are not assumed revenue;
- eligibility, applicant type, geographic scope, co-financing and deadline must be checked;
- opportunities may be relevant to consortium/NGO/community partnerships rather than direct commercial sales.

The Botswana intelligence system should now monitor:

1. National planning / budgets
2. PPRA and public procurement
3. BITC investment/export opportunities
4. BIDPA research/consultancy/tenders
5. BITRI R&D/commercialisation/technical opportunities

10. Actual customer enquiries and transactions

## Hard control

Institutional relevance is not market proof.

For every opportunity:
**SOURCE → EXACT REQUIREMENT → ELIGIBILITY → CUSTOMER/COUNTERPARTY → SUPPLIER/PARTNER → ECONOMICS → COMPLIANCE → COMMITMENT → DELIVERY → OUTCOME**

Where the opportunity is a grant:
**CALL → ELIGIBILITY → APPLICANT ROLE → PROJECT FIT → BUDGET → PARTNERS → APPLICATION AUTHORITY → AWARD → DELIVERY**

Do not represent Farouk as an NGO, bidder, consultant of record, technology partner, beneficiary, grantee or institutional partner without the required status and authorisation.

## Routing

- Policy/research → Farouk Research & Intelligence
- Technology/R&D/IoT → MOKORO / relevant specialist project + BITRI only as external institution
- Investment/export → General Dealer / sourcing / market-entry capability + BITC ecosystem
- Agriculture/value chains → AgriSage360 / AgriSolutions
- Digital productivity → MOKORO
- Community/climate/biodiversity → relevant owned research/project or authorised NGO/community partner
- Procurement → General Dealer/Sourcing or specialist authorised partner
- Grants → only where applicant eligibility and partnership structure are verified
