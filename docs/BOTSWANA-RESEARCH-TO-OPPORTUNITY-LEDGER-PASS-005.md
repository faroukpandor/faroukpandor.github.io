## Evidence controls

Research evidence is classified as **LITERATURE-SUPPORTED SIGNAL** only.

It does not establish:
- customer demand;
- willingness to pay;
- supplier capacity;
- profitability;
- regulatory approval;
- government endorsement;
- commercial readiness.
