## Qualification gate

Before committing money or making a firm promise, confirm:

- [ ] Requirement is sufficiently clear.
- [ ] Customer identity/contact is recorded appropriately.
- [ ] Quantity/specification is understood.
- [ ] Location and delivery requirements are known.
- [ ] Deadline is realistic or explicitly uncertain.
- [ ] Supplier/provider route is identified or a sourcing task is opened.
- [ ] Availability has been checked where material.
- [ ] Relevant supplier/provider evidence has been captured.
- [ ] Costs and material fees can be estimated.
- [ ] Commercial model is clear.
- [ ] Referral/commission arrangements are disclosed where relevant.
- [ ] Customer has approved the material scope/quote before procurement.
- [ ] No unsupported guarantee has been made.

## Decision

**QUALIFY** — enough information to proceed.

**HOLD** — information or verification is missing.

**DECLINE** — outside capability, unacceptable risk, or unsuitable commercial terms.

**REFER** — suitable provider can fulfil directly.

**SOURCE** — supplier/provider search required.

**QUOTE** — sufficient evidence exists to prepare a customer quotation.
