# Botswana Literature Evidence Pass 003 — AgTech, IoT, Digital Agriculture and Climate

**Research date:** 2 October 2026

## Evidence anchors

Recent peer-reviewed Botswana research explicitly describes IoT applications for farm management, including water and diesel flow/level monitoring, soil and environmental monitoring, inventory systems, animal-health/tracking systems and vaccination records. The 2025 paper presents these as technologies with potential to address documented Botswana agricultural problems. 

Research on climate-smart agriculture among 384 smallholder maize farmers in North East District found adoption is influenced by education, training, farm size, livestock ownership, group membership, extension, market access, land tenure and climate information; access to timely climate information and markets was important across practices.

A 2025 review of digital transformation/e-service delivery in Botswana's Ministry of Agriculture identifies constraints to public-sector digital service delivery. This is relevant to workflow and service-design research but is not evidence that a particular commercial product will be purchased.

Recent climate research also models Botswana livestock risks and cereal responses to climate variability, strengthening the evidence base for decision-support and risk-management tools.

## Product/service hypotheses

1. Low-cost farm monitoring kits.
2. Water/diesel level and flow monitoring.
3. Livestock tracking and health records.
4. Offline-first farm records with synchronisation when connected.
5. Climate-information and farm-risk dashboards.
6. Extension/field-agent workflow systems.
7. Inventory/vaccination/animal-event records.
8. Sensor-data collection and reporting services.
9. Agricultural data integration and exportable evidence packs.
10. IoT prototyping and orchestration using specialist technical partners.

## Design doctrine

Do not build an AI-first product merely because AI is available. Prefer deterministic/offline workflows where the problem is record keeping, calculation, monitoring or alerts. Use AI online as an optional analysis/research layer, with human review and exportable records.

## Validation requirements

For each proposed device/workflow record: farmer problem | exact variable | sensor/data source | power requirement | connectivity | device cost | installation | maintenance | calibration | data ownership | offline behaviour | replacement plan | expected benefit | buyer | willingness to pay | compliance | pilot result.

## Commercial gate

LITERATURE/TECHNOLOGY SIGNAL → FARMER PROBLEM → USER INTERVIEW → PROTOTYPE → PAID PILOT → MEASURED OUTCOME → REPEAT → PRODUCTISE

Literature and prototype feasibility do not establish willingness to pay.
