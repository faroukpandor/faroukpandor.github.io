# Africa Opportunity Signal Register 001

**Created:** 2026-10-02  
**Scope:** Botswana, Namibia and Zambia evidence generated in Africa Evidence Passes 1–3.  
**Status:** SIGNAL ONLY unless a customer, supplier, economics and compliance record is added.

## Control model

A signal enters this register when an official source identifies a development, investment, procurement, trade, value-chain or project condition that could justify a commercial research action.

A signal is **not**:
- a customer order;
- proof of market demand;
- proof of profitability;
- proof of supplier availability;
- proof of procurement eligibility;
- government endorsement;
- Farouk-owned inventory;
- permission to represent another organisation.

The next state requires downstream evidence.

## Evidence-gate template

Every signal must advance through:

**SIGNAL → PROBLEM-VERIFIED → CUSTOMER-VERIFIED → SUPPLIER-VERIFIED → ECONOMICS-VERIFIED → COMPLIANCE-VERIFIED → CUSTOMER-COMMITTED → PILOT/TRANSACTION → DELIVERED → REPEATABLE**

If a required gate fails, the record moves to HOLD, DECLINED or ARCHIVE rather than being forced forward.

## Immediate operating rule

Do not create more generic country reports until at least one signal above has progressed into:
- a named customer/project;
- a documented requirement;
- a verified supplier/provider;
- a preliminary landed-cost/economic model;
- a compliance path;
- and preferably a paid pilot or transaction.
