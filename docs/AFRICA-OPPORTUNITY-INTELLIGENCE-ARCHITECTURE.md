# Africa Opportunity Intelligence Architecture

**Status:** Phase 1 — continental architecture  
**Scope:** AU member states, regional economic communities, national development systems, historical executive/advisory signals, trade, procurement and proof-of-market evidence.

## Strategic premise

Africa research must not become 55 country reports. The system is an evidence pipeline that progressively deepens only where commercial or research evidence warrants it.

**Continental signal → regional context → country signal → local problem → customer → supplier/value chain → economics → transaction → repeatability.**

Agenda 2063's Second Ten-Year Implementation Plan covers 2024–2033 and identifies continental flagship programmes and implementation, monitoring and evaluation as core mechanisms. The research layer therefore treats continental frameworks as upstream signals rather than market proof.

## Research layers

### Layer A — Continental
- Agenda 2063 and implementation plans
- AfCFTA
- AU institutions
- AUDA-NEPAD
- continental infrastructure/trade programmes
- development-finance and trade signals

### Layer B — Regional
Track relevant RECs, including:
- SADC
- COMESA
- EAC
- ECOWAS
- ECCAS
- IGAD
- CEN-SAD
- AMU

A country may belong to more than one regional framework. Membership and applicable instruments must be verified before commercial action.

### Layer C — Country
For each country:
- historical development plans;
- current national plan;
- budget;
- investment strategy;
- industrial/agricultural/trade/digital strategies;
- executive advisory and envoy structures;
- major projects;
- procurement;
- import/export signals;
- regulatory requirements;
- investment agencies.

### Layer D — Local
Where data permits:
- province/state/region/district;
- city;
- industrial cluster;
- production area;
- logistics corridor;
- local buyer/provider network.

### Layer E — Market proof
No opportunity becomes commercially validated without customer/economic evidence.

## Historical executive/advisory research

For each country, where public evidence exists, search:
- head-of-state/head-of-government advisers;
- special advisers;
- technical/economic/legal advisers;
- special envoys;
- special representatives;
- presidential/prime-ministerial task forces;
- commissions;
- special projects;
- international missions.

Historical terminology varies. Formal title and functional role must be stored separately.

## Market-proof gates

**SIGNAL** — documented policy/project/market signal.

**PROBLEM-VERIFIED** — underlying problem independently confirmed.

**CUSTOMER-VERIFIED** — identifiable potential buyer/user confirmed.

**SUPPLIER-VERIFIED** — realistic supply/delivery route confirmed.

**ECONOMICS-VERIFIED** — cost, price, contribution and compliance model tested.

**PILOT-PAID** — actual payment.

**REPEATABLE** — repeat purchase, renewal or credible recurring pipeline.

**SCALE-READY** — replicable process with evidence and controls.

Continental or governmental alignment cannot substitute for these gates.

## Regional priority logic

Botswana is the laboratory.

Phase 1: Botswana deep research.

Phase 2: SADC corridor research, prioritising cross-border opportunities that can be tested from Botswana.

Phase 3: selected COMESA/EAC/ECOWAS/ECCAS/IGAD corridors based on actual signals.

Phase 4: automated continental coverage with human deep dives only when evidence gates justify them.

This prevents building a large information library without commercial validation.

## Automation

Automate:
- official-source monitoring;
- document retrieval dates and hashes;
- change detection;
- source classification;
- deduplication;
- stale-evidence alerts;
- opportunity inbox creation;
- cross-country/sector tagging;
- market-proof ledger calculations;
- weekly opportunity reports.

Do not automate:
- political preference inference;
- Government endorsement claims;
- procurement eligibility decisions;
- purchases;
- tender submissions;
- regulated approvals;
- customer commitments;
- unverified market claims.

## Resilience

Every important signal should have:
1. primary source;
2. alternate official/credible source;
3. retrieval date;
4. evidence status;
5. portable local record;
6. manual fallback.

Loss of a website, API, AI provider, repository or automation workflow must not stop a live customer transaction.

## Minimum country record

`country_id`, `country_name`, `AU_region`, `REC_memberships`, `historical_plan_sources`, `current_plan`, `budget`, `investment_agency`, `trade_agencies`, `executive_advisory_sources`, `envoy_sources`, `procurement_sources`, `sector_signals`, `trade_corridors`, `regulatory_sources`, `market_evidence`, `opportunities`, `last_verified`, `review_due`.

## Decision rule

**Research depth follows evidence.**

A country receives deeper research when:
- a customer need appears;
- a supplier relationship appears;
- a trade corridor becomes commercially relevant;
- a recurring procurement category appears;
- a Farouk-owned capability maps strongly to a documented problem;
- a paid pilot or transaction creates evidence.

The objective is not to know everything about Africa. It is to systematically discover, verify and execute legitimate opportunities while building a reusable intelligence asset.
