# Botswana NDP Historical-to-Current Opportunity Matrix

**Status:** Research and commercial-intelligence asset — 2 October 2026  
**Scope:** Botswana development planning from the Transitional Plan through NDP 12, with current NTS/BETP/2026-27 alignment.  
**Purpose:** Convert historical development-planning continuity into testable commercial opportunities without implying Government endorsement, eligibility, procurement status, funding, licensing or participation.

## 1. Operating principle

Botswana's National Planning Commission states that NDPs guide national development through strategies, programmes, projects and initiatives; District Development Plans precede national planning, and preparation is consultative. NDP 12 is the current five-year plan and is aligned to the National Transformation Strategy. [1]

Use:

**Persistent problem → historical response → unresolved/current bottleneck → current policy expression → customer → commercial offer → evidence → measurable outcome**

Policy relevance is a discovery filter, not proof of demand.

## 3. Cross-plan opportunity families

### A. Water and resource efficiency
Historical continuity: water access and management recur from the foundational planning era into current infrastructure/resource priorities.

Potential offers:
- water-use assessment;
- sourcing of water-efficiency products;
- irrigation/input coordination;
- maintenance/provider coordination;
- farm water records through AgriSage360.

Do not market engineering, plumbing or regulated services as personally performed unless appropriately qualified/licensed.

### B. Agricultural productivity and value chains
Historical continuity: agriculture, rural productivity and market access recur across plans.

Potential chain:
**farmer problem → requirement → input/provider → production support → aggregation → processing/customisation → market access → repeat order**

Relevant assets: AgriSage360, AgriSolutions, General Dealer, research/intelligence.

### C. Manufacturing and local value addition
Historical continuity: productive capacity and manufacturing recur, with current emphasis on value chains and competitiveness.

Potential chain:
**customer requirement → source blank/input → local customisation → quality control → packaging → delivery**

Relevant assets: General Dealer, merchandise/branding coordination, Brandcraft where applicable.

### D. Digital productivity
Historical continuity: implementation/results management evolves into current digital transformation.

Potential chain:
**manual workflow → diagnose → lightweight PWA/static tool → offline-capable records → evidence → improvement**

Relevant assets: Mokoro, digital operations, portable PWA architecture.

### E. Trade, sourcing and logistics
Historical continuity: market access, transport and communications remain foundational.

Potential chain:
**demand → source → verify → landed/direct cost → quote → procure → logistics → evidence → reconciliation**

Relevant assets: General Dealer, AutoLink, supplier/provider network.

### F. Export and regional market access
Historical continuity: diversification and global competitiveness evolve into current export-oriented transformation.

Potential chain:
**local product/service → quality/evidence → target market → buyer verification → compliance → pilot → repeat export**

Relevant assets: EverythingCity, research/intelligence, sourcing and market-entry capability.

## 4. Commercial scoring gate

An opportunity enters the active pipeline only when it has enough evidence across:

1. **Problem:** identifiable customer problem.
2. **Customer:** identifiable buyer/user.
3. **Evidence:** credible evidence of demand.
4. **Solution:** feasible service/product/coordination route.
5. **Economics:** positive contribution after direct/landed costs.
6. **Capability:** Farouk-owned capability or clearly identified partner.
7. **Compliance:** no unresolved regulatory barrier.
8. **Delivery:** realistic supplier/provider/logistics route.
9. **Repeatability:** plausible repeat or recurring demand.
10. **Strategic relevance:** national/district alignment where applicable.

Policy alignment cannot compensate for failure of the first nine gates.

## 6. Governance

- Government documents are evidence sources, not endorsements.
- Do not claim Government partnership, eligibility, procurement status or funding without documentary proof.
- Do not label a business opportunity as viable merely because it appears in a national plan.
- Keep client/employer/third-party enterprises separate from Farouk-owned assets.
- Historical relevance must not be presented as a current Government commitment.
- Every material current-policy claim should retain its source and retrieval date.
- Commercial decisions must remain evidence-led.
