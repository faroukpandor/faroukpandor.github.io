# Botswana Peer-Reviewed and Scholarly Evidence Architecture

**Status:** Initial source audit — 2 October 2026

## Important scope statement

This is **not** a claim that every Botswana-related peer-reviewed publication has been exhaustively retrieved.

Botswana scholarship is distributed across institutional repositories, Botswana journals, African journal indexes, international bibliographic databases, theses/dissertations, conference proceedings, books/chapters, government research and technical reports. Some international databases are subscription-controlled and cannot be exhaustively enumerated from open web discovery alone.

The objective is therefore to establish a reproducible literature-ingestion system and deepen searches according to commercial/research relevance.

## Core Botswana scholarly repositories and publication systems

### BUAN ResearchHub
Searchable scholarly database containing:
- Botswana Journal of Agriculture and Applied Sciences;
- Faculty of Agribusiness, Education and Extension;
- Faculty of Agriculture;
- Faculty of Animal and Veterinary Sciences;
- Faculty of Natural Resources;
- Faculty of Sciences;
- theses and dissertations.

Current repository evidence includes 2026 work on medicinal plants from Botswana.

Source: https://researchhub.buan.ac.bw/

### BIUST Repository (BIUSTRE)
Contains:
- research articles;
- electronic theses and dissertations;
- books/chapters;
- conference/symposium/workshop/seminar works.

Its research-article collection includes peer-reviewed publications and preprints. The repository currently exposes Botswana-tagged records and subjects including climate change, machine learning, biomass, energy and optimisation.

Source: https://repository.biust.ac.bw/

### University of Botswana / Botswana Notes and Records
Botswana Notes and Records provides a substantial scholarly archive covering history, society, economics, environment, agriculture, biodiversity, culture, governance and other Botswana subjects.

The archive includes recent volumes as well as historical scholarship.

Source: https://journals.ub.bw/index.php/bnr/

### African Journals Online
AJOL should be searched systematically for Botswana-related articles across agriculture, economics, technology, health, natural resources, social sciences and other fields.

AJOL currently lists Botswana Journal of Economics and Botswana Journal of Technology among its journals, while older regional agricultural archives contain Botswana-specific studies.

Source: https://www.ajol.info/

### PubMed
Use PubMed for Botswana biomedical, veterinary, One Health, epidemiology, ethnomedicine, antimicrobial resistance, nutrition and related literature.

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/

## Evidence-to-opportunity extraction

For each relevant publication extract:

- citation;
- DOI/identifier;
- publication year;
- Botswana locality/population;
- research question;
- method;
- sample/data;
- key finding;
- limitation;
- unresolved problem;
- technology/process identified;
- resource identified;
- value-chain implication;
- customer affected;
- supplier/capability implication;
- policy relevance;
- commercial hypothesis;
- Farouk-owned asset route;
- evidence confidence;
- whether current or historical;
- last verified;
- source URL.

## Critical distinction

A peer-reviewed finding is evidence about a research question. It is not automatically:
- market demand;
- product-market fit;
- regulatory approval;
- safety approval;
- commercial feasibility;
- customer willingness to pay;
- supplier capacity;
- government endorsement.

Literature must therefore feed the same market-proof gates:

**LITERATURE SIGNAL → CURRENT PROBLEM → CUSTOMER → SOLUTION → SUPPLIER/CAPABILITY → ECONOMICS → COMPLIANCE → PILOT → OUTCOME → REPEAT**

## Priority research examples identified during initial audit

- BUAN repository currently contains Botswana medicinal-plant research, including a 2026 paper on metabolite profiles, antioxidant and antibacterial activity of three medicinal plants.
- Recent peer-reviewed work examines One Health governance and climate pressure in Botswana.
- Peer-reviewed research documents structured grazing/Herding for Health outcomes in the Okavango Delta.
- Research addresses conservation agriculture adoption constraints in Botswana.
- Literature covers Indigenous ecological knowledge and climate adaptation.
- Earlier agricultural literature includes sweet sorghum, forage grasses, bambara groundnut, crop residues, oyster mushrooms, agroforestry and soil fertility.
- Botswana Notes and Records contains extensive social, historical, environmental and economic scholarship.

## Literature recursion rule

Do not attempt to read every paper indiscriminately.

Use a funnel:

**Broad retrieval → Botswana relevance → peer-review verification → evidence extraction → thematic synthesis → unresolved problem → current evidence → customer hypothesis → commercial validation**

Deep review should increase where literature reveals:
- repeated unresolved problems;
- measurable economic loss;
- resource/value-chain potential;
- technology gap;
- import dependency;
- safety/efficacy evidence;
- scalable production method;
- customer pain;
- policy/market change.

## Research integrity

- Do not treat abstracts as full-paper evidence where methods/results are needed.
- Do not confuse thesis findings with peer-reviewed validation.
- Do not treat repeated citation as independent confirmation.
- Record conflicting findings.
- Record publication date and study period.
- Distinguish Botswana-specific evidence from Southern African/general evidence.
- Do not infer endemicity, rarity, threat status or commercial safety from absence of literature.
- For medicinal/nutraceutical/veterinary claims, require appropriate scientific, safety and regulatory evidence before commercialisation.
