## Trust and safety

Customer accounts and data remain customer-owned. Do not collect passwords or store credentials in a register. Classify information by sensitivity, use least privilege and MFA where available, agree retention/deletion and handover, and document backup/restore tests. Review provider privacy, security, terms and suitability before using it for customer data. Never claim zero data loss or guaranteed compliance.
