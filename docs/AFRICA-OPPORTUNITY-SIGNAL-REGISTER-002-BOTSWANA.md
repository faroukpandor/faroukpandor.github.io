# Africa Opportunity Signal Register 002 — Botswana Downstream Controls

**Verified:** 2026-10-02

## AFR-SIG-008 — Botswana procurement digitisation implementation
- Country: Botswana
- Source: Ministry of Finance official procurement notice.
- Signal: an official notice dated 2 October 2026 concerns a service contract to supply, design, develop and implement an e-Government Procurement system.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- State: SIGNAL.
- Commercial mode: specialist digital procurement capability / authorised partnership only.
- Control: tender publication does not establish Farouk eligibility, consortium membership, technical qualification or award.
- Next action: assess only after complete tender documents, eligibility, deadline, mandatory experience and authorised delivery capacity are verified.
- Source: https://www.finance.gov.bw/index.php?Itemid=201&catid=38&id=339&option=com_content&view=article

## AFR-SIG-009 — Botswana recurring food and beverage import research
- Country: Botswana
- Source: Statistics Botswana.
- Signal: Statistics Botswana maintains recurring monthly food and beverage import releases, including June 2026.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- State: SIGNAL.
- Commercial mode: SOURCE-TO-ORDER / LOCAL-SUPPLY DISCOVERY / IMPORT-TO-ORDER.
- Control: aggregate import value is not proof that a particular product is commercially attractive.
- Next action: obtain product-level data from the official publication/data portal, cross-reference Government's local-opportunity list, then test named customer requirements.
- Source: https://www.statsbots.org.bw/botswana-food-beverage-imports-june-2026-0

## AFR-SIG-010 — Botswana customs landed-cost control
- Country: Botswana
- Source: Botswana Unified Revenue Service.
- Signal: BURS publishes general VAT/customs treatment by origin and a customs tariff schedule; exact treatment remains product-specific.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- State: CONTROL-SIGNAL.
- Commercial mode: all import-to-order and import resale.
- Control: supplier price must not be represented as Botswana landed price.
- Next action: attach tariff classification, origin, freight, insurance, duties/taxes and clearing costs before customer commitment.
- Source: https://www.burs.org.bw/?Itemid=213&id=58&option=com_content&view=article
