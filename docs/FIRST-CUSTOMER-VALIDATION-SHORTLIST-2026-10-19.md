# First Customer Validation — Prospect and Channel Shortlist

**Status:** Research shortlist only. No outreach sent; no relationship, need, interest, budget or willingness to pay is established.
**Date researched:** 2026-10-19
**Purpose:** Test whether a bounded website/digital-presence review or SME workflow assessment solves a real problem.
**Rule:** Verify current contact route and fit before any outreach. Do not infer a defect from the existence of a website. Respect applicable contact, privacy and platform rules.

## Recommended experiment

- Interview 5–8 qualified decision-makers before expanding the list.
- Lead with a question about their current enquiry/customer workflow, not a generic pitch.
- Offer a short discovery conversation; only propose a paid audit if a specific need is confirmed.
- Record source URL, date checked, role fit, actual response, objections and next step.
- Do not send bulk messages or imply a previous relationship.

## Outreach script — draft only, do not send automatically

**Subject:** Question about your online enquiry/order workflow

Hello [Name/Team],

I’m validating a small, fixed-scope service for Botswana businesses that reviews one online customer journey—such as a product enquiry, booking or wholesale order—and returns a short, evidence-based list of improvements.

Before I offer it, may I ask how you currently handle [specific workflow from a verified public source] and whether there is any part you would want to make simpler? If it is not a current priority, no problem.

Regards,  
[Your name / approved business identity]

## Qualification questions

1. Is the contact the decision-maker or can they direct the enquiry appropriately?
2. Is the problem current and important enough to spend time or money on?
3. What do they do today, and what does it cost in time, lost enquiries or rework?
4. What would a useful deliverable need to include?
5. What data/access/permissions would be needed, and can the scope avoid sensitive data?
6. What budget/process would apply to a small pilot?
7. What evidence or references would they need before buying?
8. What would make this a clear “not now” or “not a fit”?

## Stop / adjust rules

- If a business is inactive, its website/contact is stale, or fit cannot be established, remove it from the active shortlist.
- If several conversations reveal no urgent problem, change the offer rather than increasing outreach volume.
- Do not claim an audit found defects before testing the site.
- Do not use unverified customer logos, case studies, endorsements or success claims.
- Do not store personal contact details in a public repository. Keep only public business contact routes needed for the task and move any non-public CRM data to an authorised private system.
