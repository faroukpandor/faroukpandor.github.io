# Africa Evidence Pass 3 — Zambia Country and Corridor Evidence

**Verified:** 2026-10-02  
**Purpose:** Add a second SADC country with current official investment, trade-facilitation and corridor evidence. This is an evidence pass, not a country ranking.

## Zambia

### ZAM-EVD-001 — Current national planning framework
- Source: Republic of Zambia, Eighth National Development Plan (8NDP), 2022–2026.
- Fact: the 8NDP includes strategies to facilitate domestic and international trade, link SMEs with larger local and regional value chains, improve market information, digitise trade-facilitation processes and improve access to finance for production and exports.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- Commercial use: trade/value-chain/digitalisation signal only.
- Source: https://www.cabinet.gov.zm/newsite/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/8NDP-2022-2026.pdf

### ZAM-EVD-002 — Current public investment source
- Source: Zambia Ministry of Finance and National Planning.
- Fact: a 2026 Public Investment Plan is published as an official investment-planning resource.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- Commercial use: project discovery and downstream supplier research; individual project demand and procurement status require verification.
- Source: https://www.mofnp.gov.zm/?wpdmpro=2026-public-investment-plan

### ZAM-EVD-003 — Investment Deal Room
- Source: Zambia Development Agency Investment Deal Room.
- Fact: the official platform presents a verified-participant investment pipeline and, on its current page, reports 417 fund seekers, 55 funders and 379 projects, with priority sectors including agribusiness, manufacturing, construction, mining, energy, tourism, water supply and ICT.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- Commercial use: lead/project discovery and partnership research. Platform participation is not evidence that a particular project is suitable for Farouk or that financing will occur.
- Source: https://zidr.zda.org.zm/

### ZAM-EVD-004 — Manufacturing project signals
- Source: Zambia Development Agency manufacturing sector.
- Fact: ZDA publishes specific project opportunities including an organic fertiliser manufacturing project seeking private investment and a tomato processing project seeking private investment.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- Commercial use: project/partner discovery signal. Investment requirements, diligence, permits, counterparties and economics must be independently verified before engagement.
- Source: https://zda.org.zm/manufacturing/

### ZAM-EVD-005 — Import/customs pathway
- Source: Zambia Revenue Authority.
- Fact: ZRA describes freighted commercial imports above USD 2,000 as electronically declared and identifies invoices, transport documents, contracts and permits/certificates where applicable; it also states that applicable duties and taxes are payable and that value for duty purposes is based on CIF-related costs and incidental costs up to the port of entry.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL.
- Commercial use: cross-border landed-cost and compliance workflow.
- Source: https://www.zra.org.zm/importation-of-goods/

### ZAM-EVD-006 — Lobito Corridor investment pipeline
- Source: Zambia Presidential Delivery Unit, 5 June 2026.
- Fact: the PDU reported that the 2025 EU-Zambia Lobito Corridor Business Forum generated an emerging investment and partnership pipeline across agriculture, energy, manufacturing, critical raw materials, infrastructure/logistics and green economy, with reported opportunities including partnerships, financing arrangements, joint ventures, offtake agreements, supply-chain linkages, technology transfer and export-market opportunities.
- Evidence class: VERIFIED-PRIMARY.
- Commercial use: corridor/value-chain signal requiring project-level due diligence; it is not proof of a Farouk opportunity or customer demand.
- Source: https://www.pdu.gov.zm/blog/lobito-corridor-forum-generates-strong-investment-pipeline-as-stakeholders-shift-focus-to-implementation

## Commercial routing hypotheses

These records justify deeper research in:
- agribusiness and agro-processing;
- manufacturing inputs/equipment;
- regional trade facilitation and sourcing;
- logistics/corridor support;
- digital/business services;
- selected project-partnership and supplier-linkage opportunities.

They do **not** justify purchasing inventory, representing a project, promising supply, claiming government endorsement, or quoting a customer without a verified requirement.

## Next evidence requirements

For any Zambia opportunity to advance:
1. identify the actual buyer/project promoter;
2. verify current status and contact route;
3. capture exact product/service requirement;
4. identify at least one credible supplier/provider;
5. obtain price/lead-time/logistics evidence;
6. determine customs, tax and permit pathway;
7. calculate landed contribution;
8. obtain customer commitment or paid pilot;
9. record outcome and repeatability.

## Control

No country ranking is created. Zambia is included because the current evidence provides concrete project, investment, trade-facilitation and corridor records that can be tested downstream. This does not imply that Zambia is more attractive than any other SADC country.
