PRIVACY, TERMS & EVIDENCE DISCLOSURES

Simple site. Clear data rules.

This website is a static GitHub Pages routing and evidence hub operated by Farouk Pandor (Gaborone, Botswana). It runs without a backend database, user accounts or advertising trackers.

Enquiry intake & consent

The enquiry form on the homepage prepares a structured summary on your device so you can send it via your email client, copy it into WhatsApp/email or save a portable JSON copy. Nothing is stored on any server by this website unless and until you actively send your message to faroukpandor@gmail.com. Information sent is used strictly to evaluate and reply to your enquiry.

Cookies & analytics status

No third-party analytics script, advertising pixel or tracking cookie is installed on these pages. Client-side tools such as the Website Health Check optionally save your draft checklist in browser localStorage on your own device only, with a one-click reset button, and never transmit data elsewhere.

Ownership & portfolio boundaries

Public links point only to verified public destinations in the portfolio and the read-only control catalogue. Client, employer and third-party repositories are never exposed or edited without explicit written authorisation, and private repositories remain unlisted here.

Evidence & honest claims

Public pages describe working scope, live static tools and verified governance baselines without fabricating testimonials, regulatory/organic certifications, revenue forecasts or search guarantees. Where an automated monitor source reports an error (such as cached signal snapshots), that status is shown openly rather than smoothed over.

Affiliate links & commissions

No affiliate or sponsored links are currently placed on this website. The dedicated Affiliate & Editorial Disclosure explains the rules that will apply if a commercial relationship is added. Applying to an affiliate programme does not mean an account has been approved. If an affiliate link is added here, it will be identified close to the recommendation; I may earn a commission if you buy through it, without an added charge from the merchant for using that link. A commission is not evidence of independent testing or product suitability. Material limitations and alternatives should be described honestly.
