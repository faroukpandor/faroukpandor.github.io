03 / CLIENT & THIRD-PARTY WORK

Experience is not the same as ownership.

Some deployments in the wider working portfolio support clients or other parties. They are deliberately not mixed into the owned-product showcase. Client names, systems and links should be published as case studies only where the relevant permission and agreed scope allow it.

Clear boundaries

Client and employer assets remain separate from Farouk-owned ventures. Access to a repository, contribution to a project, or a public deployment URL does not establish ownership or permission to make changes.

Evidence before claims

Project status, customer outcomes, partner authorisations, certifications, revenue and production readiness are stated only when supported by appropriate evidence. Links are provided for discovery, not as a guarantee of uptime or functionality.
