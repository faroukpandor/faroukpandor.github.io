COMMERCIAL TRANSPARENCY

Affiliate & editorial disclosure.

A recommendation should help a reader make a better-informed decision. Any commercial relationship must be visible, and it must never be presented as independent evidence.

Current position

No affiliate or sponsored placements are currently published on this personal website. This page sets the disclosure standard that will apply before any such link or placement is introduced. A general policy does not mean that a particular page contains an affiliate link.

How commercial links will be identified

An affiliate or referral link will be labelled clearly before or beside the link, using wording such as Affiliate link or Referral link. Sponsored articles, paid placements, gifted products and other material benefits will be identified in the relevant content, not hidden in a footer or in this general notice.

Where a link is not commercial, it will not be described as an affiliate link. Where the relationship is uncertain, the link will not be presented as an approved partnership until its terms have been checked.

Commissions and other compensation

If a reader follows a qualifying affiliate or referral link and completes an eligible action, a merchant or programme may pay a commission or other compensation. The amount and qualifying conditions depend on that programme. Any statement about whether using a link changes the price will be made only where the relevant merchant terms support it.

Compensation is not evidence that a product is suitable, available in Botswana, independently tested or the best option. Readers should check the provider's current price, eligibility, delivery coverage, terms and privacy practices before acting.

Editorial independence and product claims

Commercial relationships do not purchase a favourable conclusion, a higher ranking or a guaranteed recommendation. Where comparisons are published, the criteria and important limitations should be explained. Paid content will be distinguished from independent editorial material.

I will not claim to have bought, used, tested or verified a product or service unless that work was actually performed. Manufacturer specifications, provider statements, desktop research and hands-on observations will be distinguished rather than blended into one unsupported claim.

Corrections and questions

Prices, availability, commission terms and product details can change. Readers should use current provider information for final decisions. To report an error, request a correction or ask about a commercial relationship, email faroukpandor@gmail.com and identify the page and claim concerned.

Ownership, privacy and scope

Links to client, employer or third-party work do not imply ownership, endorsement or authority to act for those parties. Personal information is handled as described in the Privacy & Disclosures notice. Professional engagements and regulated matters remain subject to their own written scope and the involvement of appropriately qualified providers.
